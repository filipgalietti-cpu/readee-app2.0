# Silent-E — Lesson Script (redesign, "from the top")

_A pedagogy-first script for the RF.K.3b silent-E lesson. Purpose: get the TEACHING right on
paper before building. This doc is (1) Jennifer's red-line doc — she owns the pedagogy;
(2) Claude Design's motion brief — it owns the animation; (3) the build spec.
K rule: teach by SHOWING + short spoken lines + MEANING. Richness = more teaching moments,
not more words._

## Why the first version felt hollow (and what fixes it)
It taught the *mechanic* (add E → long vowel) but nothing else. The four fixes:
1. **Ground every word in MEANING + a picture.** cap = a hat; cape = a superhero cape. When the
   word changes, the *picture changes too* — that's the "aha," and it's what makes it stick.
2. **Read in CONTEXT.** The kid reads a new word inside a real sentence, not just in isolation.
3. **Systematic practice across vowels** (a_e, i_e, o_e, u_e), not just one.
4. **Say it aloud** (pronunciation, Azure) — hear it → build it → say it.

## How animation is described (not coded)
Each scene declares **intent** with semantic tags; Claude Design decides the actual motion/feel:
`entrance` (float-in, pop, slide) · `transform` (morph, snap-together) · `emphasis` (glow, spotlight)
· `success` (burst, small-celebration). Below, each scene lists its **Motion intent**.

## Objectives (what the kid actually learns)
- A vowel has a **short** sound (ă) and a **long** sound (ā = "says its name").
- A silent **E** at the end is **quiet** but makes the vowel **long** (the V-C-e pattern).
- Read & build silent-E words across **multiple vowels**.
- **Spelling changes meaning**: cap → cape is a different thing.
- **Read** silent-E words in a sentence, and **say** them aloud.

## Scene-by-scene

### 1 · Hook + meaning
- **Screen:** a picture of a **cap** (hat) above the word `CAP`.
- **Kid does:** tap it. **Hears:** "cap."
- **Teach:** "This is a cap — you wear it on your head."
- **Motion intent:** `entrance: pop` on the picture; gentle `emphasis: glow` on tap.

### 2 · The magic (meaning + sound both change) ← the heart of the lesson
- **Screen:** a silent **E** floats in and lands at the end → `CAP` becomes `CAPE`, and the
  **picture morphs from a hat into a superhero cape**.
- **Hears:** "cap… cape!"
- **Teach (short):** "Watch! Add an E… now it's a CAPE!"
- **Why it matters:** the kid *sees* that changing the spelling changes both the **sound** and the
  **meaning**. That's the concept, shown — not told.
- **Motion intent:** E `entrance: float-in` → `transform: snap-together`; picture `transform: morph`
  (hat→cape); vowel `emphasis: glow`.

### 3 · The rule (shown, K-simple)
- **Screen:** tiles `CĂP → CĀPE`; the E tagged **"silent"**, the vowel tagged **"says its name."**
- **Narration (short):** "The E is quiet. It makes the a say its name — ā."
- **Motion intent:** `emphasis: spotlight` sweeps E (dim) then vowel (bright).

### 4–5 · Model across vowels (meaning + picture each)
- `KIT` (a kit/box) → `KITE` (a flying kite) — picture morphs, hear it.
- `TUB` (bathtub) → `TUBE` (a tube) — picture morphs, hear it.
- **Teach:** "Same magic E — every time!" (systematic: a_e, i_e, u_e)
- **Motion intent:** same as scene 2, faster; picture `transform: morph` each.

### 6–7 · Guided (we do)
- **Kid does:** adds the E to make the word; on success the **picture reveals** the meaning.
- Words: `KIT→KITE`, `HOP→HOPE` (with hope maybe abstract → use `NOTE`/`ROSE` w/ picture).
- **Coach on wrong:** "Try the silent E!"
- **Motion intent:** `transform: snap-together` on correct; `success: small-celebration`; wrong = gentle nudge.

### 8 · Fix the mistake (meaning-grounded)
- **Screen:** Bunny wrote `CUB` (a bear cub, picture) but meant `CUBE` (an ice cube).
- **Kid does:** add the E → picture morphs cub → ice cube.
- **Motion intent:** `transform: morph`; `success: burst`.

### 9 · Read it in context ← the missing piece
- **Screen:** a decodable sentence with the new word, e.g. **"I like my kite."** (picture of the kite).
- **Kid does:** tap each word to read along; the silent-E word highlights.
- **Teach:** hearing/reading the word in a real sentence, not just alone.
- **Motion intent:** `emphasis: underline-sweep` across words as read; `glow` on the VCe word.

### 10 · Say it (pronunciation — Azure)
- **Kid does:** read a silent-E word **aloud**; scored by Azure (Luna engine).
- **No-reveal:** audio does NOT pronounce it first (honest check). Always a "continue" escape.
- **Motion intent:** mic `emphasis: pulse` while listening; `success: burst` on good read.

### 11 · Sort (apply)
- **Kid does:** sort words by the sound they hear — short (ă) vs long (ā).
- **Motion intent:** `transform: move-to-bucket` (words fly into buckets); wrong bucket `gentle-shake`.

### 12 · Challenge (independent, no reveal)
- **Screen:** "Make the long word." from `PIN` (no picture hint, no answer word shown).
- **Motion intent:** minimal — this is assessment; keep it calm.

### 13 · Celebrate
- **Screen:** the words they built, `mastery-celebration`.
- **Restate (short):** "Silent E makes the vowel say its name!"

## What changed vs. the hollow version
| Added | Why it kills "hollow" |
|---|---|
| Picture + meaning on every word; picture **morphs** with the word | phonics grounded in meaning — the core fix |
| Systematic across vowels (a_e, i_e, o_e/u_e) | it's a *pattern*, taught as one |
| **Read in a sentence** (context) | reading, not just letter-flipping |
| Say-it-aloud (Azure) | hear → build → say, full loop |
| Semantic **motion intent** per scene | Claude Design animates from a clear brief |
