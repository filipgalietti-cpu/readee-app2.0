## [teach] heading: "How Do Facts Connect?"
  AUDIO: Sometimes one fact helps explain another fact.
    SCREEN: Facts connect
  AUDIO: Like: The sun is hot. Plants need the sun to grow. See? The sun connects to the plants!
    SCREEN: Sun → plants grow
  AUDIO: Connections help us understand why things happen and how things are related!
    SCREEN: Understand why things happen

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One!"
  AUDIO: Listen to these facts and find the connection!
  AUDIO: Caterpillars eat lots of leaves.
    SCREEN: Caterpillars eat lots of leaves.
  AUDIO: Then caterpillars turn into butterflies!
    SCREEN: Then: butterflies
  AUDIO: The connection? Eating leaves helps the caterpillar grow so it can become a butterfly!
    SCREEN: Connection? / Eating leaves helps grow

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: There are a few special words that tell us how ideas connect. Each one tells you something different!
    SCREEN: Special connecting words
  AUDIO: because. Tells you WHY!
    SCREEN: because → WHY
  AUDIO: so. Tells you WHAT HAPPENED!
    SCREEN: so → WHAT HAPPENED
  AUDIO: then. Tells you WHAT COMES NEXT!
    SCREEN: then → WHAT NEXT
  AUDIO: You're a connection master!
    SCREEN: Connection master!
