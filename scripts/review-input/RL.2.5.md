# RL.2.5 — The Shape of a Story  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Stories Have Shape"
  AUDIO: Every story has a shape. A beginning, middle, and end.
    SCREEN: Beginning / Middle / End
  AUDIO: The shape helps us follow along.
    SCREEN: Shape helps us follow
  AUDIO: Let us look at each part.

## [teach] heading: "The Beginning"
  AUDIO: The beginning introduces us. Who is the story about? Where are they?
    SCREEN: Beginning introduces us
  AUDIO: It sets up the whole story.
    SCREEN: Sets up the story

## [teach] heading: "The Middle and End"
  AUDIO: The middle is where most of the action happens.
    SCREEN: Middle: most action
  AUDIO: There is usually a problem or a big event.
    SCREEN: Problem or big event
  AUDIO: The end wraps things up. How did it all turn out?
    SCREEN: End wraps things up

## [tip] heading: "Map the Shape"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: As you read, ask: am I in the beginning, middle, or end?
    SCREEN: Beginning / Middle / End?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to see the shape of a story!
