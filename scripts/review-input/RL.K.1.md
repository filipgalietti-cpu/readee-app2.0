# RL.K.1 — Finding Key Details  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Learn About Key Details!"
  AUDIO: Hey there, friend! Are you ready to learn something super cool today?
  AUDIO: We're going to learn about key details! Key details are like special clues hiding inside every story.
    SCREEN: Key Details are Special Clues!
  AUDIO: They tell us who is in the story, what happens, and where it all takes place. Let's go find some!
    SCREEN: Who? / What? / Where?

## [teach] heading: "What Are Key Details?"
  AUDIO: So, what are key details? They're the really important parts of a story.
    SCREEN: The important parts of a story
  AUDIO: Think of it like this. When you read a story, ask yourself: Who is the story about?
    SCREEN: Who?
  AUDIO: What happened in the story?
    SCREEN: What?
  AUDIO: And finally: Where did it happen? When you can answer those three questions, you found the key details! Pretty cool, right?
    SCREEN: Where?

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Okay, let's try one together! Here's a little story.
  AUDIO: Max the dog ran to the park. He played with a red ball.
    SCREEN: Max the dog ran to the park. He played with a red ball.
  AUDIO: Now let's find the key details! Who is the story about? That's right — Max the dog!
    SCREEN: Who? / Max the dog!
  AUDIO: Where did it happen? At the park!
    SCREEN: Where? / The park!
  AUDIO: And what did Max do? He played with a red ball! See? You just found all three key details. Amazing job!
    SCREEN: What? / Played with a red ball!

## [tip] heading: "A Secret Trick!"
  AUDIO: Here's a secret trick that works every single time.
  AUDIO: After you read a story, ask yourself three magic questions.
    SCREEN: 3 Magic Questions
  AUDIO: Who is in the story? What happened? And where did it happen? If you remember those three questions, you'll find the key details every time!
    SCREEN: Who? / What? / Where?
