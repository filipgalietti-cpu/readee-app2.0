# RF.K.2b — Clapping Syllables  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Clap Syllables!"
  AUDIO: Words have special beats called syllables! And we can clap them out!
    SCREEN: Syllables = beats
  AUDIO: Like the word 'tiger' — ti-ger! That's two claps, two syllables!
    SCREEN: Tiger: two syllables
  AUDIO: Let's learn to clap out syllables in words!

## [teach] heading: "What Are Syllables?"
  AUDIO: A syllable is a part of a word that has one beat.
  AUDIO: Some words have one beat — like 'cat.' Just one clap! Cat!
    SCREEN: Cat has 1 clap
  AUDIO: Some words have two beats — like 'apple.' Ap-ple! Two claps!
    SCREEN: Ap-ple has 2 claps
  AUDIO: And some have three or more — like 'banana.' Ba-na-na! Three claps!
    SCREEN: Ba-na-na has 3 claps

## [example] heading: "Let's Clap Together!"
  AUDIO: Let's try it! Clap along with me!
  AUDIO: Dog. One clap! Dog has one syllable.
    SCREEN: Dog has 1
  AUDIO: Mon-key. Two claps! Monkey has two syllables!
    SCREEN: Mon-key has 2
  AUDIO: El-e-phant. Three claps! Elephant has three syllables! You're clapping champ!
    SCREEN: El-e-phant has 3

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Put your hand under your chin and say a word!
  AUDIO: Every time your chin drops down, that's one syllable! Your chin knows the beats!
    SCREEN: Chin drops has 1 syllable
  AUDIO: Try it with your name! How many syllables does YOUR name have?

## [interactive] heading: "Your Turn!"
  FORK kind=tap Q(spoken): Your turn, superstar! Let's clap out the word 'rabbit'. How many syllables do you hear?
  on-screen: How many syllables? | ['One', 'Two', 'Three'] ✓Two

## [practice-intro] heading: "Time to Practice!"
  AUDIO: You're a syllable-clapping superstar!
  AUDIO: Let's practice counting syllables in some words. Get your clapping hands ready!
    SCREEN: Let's clap the syllables!
