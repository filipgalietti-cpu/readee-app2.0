# RL.3.7 — Pictures and Mood  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Pictures Set the Mood"
  AUDIO: Hey there, amazing reader! Today, we're going to explore how pictures in a story do more than just show us what's happening.
    SCREEN: Hey there / A / Mazing reader
  AUDIO: They also help set the mood, or the feeling, of the story.
    SCREEN: Set the mood or feeling
  AUDIO: Let's discover how illustrations can make us feel happy, sad, or even excited!

## [teach] heading: "Bright vs Dark"
  AUDIO: Bright colors feel happy or exciting.
    SCREEN: Bright = happy, exciting
  AUDIO: Dark or cold colors feel sad or scary.
    SCREEN: Dark/cold = sad, scary

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Picture this illustration.
  AUDIO: A sunny meadow with bright flowers and a smiling, dancing girl.
    SCREEN: A sunny meadow with bright flowers and a smiling, dancing girl.
  AUDIO: Are the colors bright or dark? They are bright and sunny!
    SCREEN: Bright or dark? / Bright colors.
  AUDIO: So what mood does it set? A happy, cheerful feeling!
    SCREEN: What mood? / Happy and cheerful.
  AUDIO: Bright colors made a happy mood. You read the picture's feeling!

## [example] heading: "Storm on the Page"
  AUDIO: A stormy picture tells us the mood is tense.
    SCREEN: Stormy picture: tense mood
  AUDIO: We feel the tension just by looking!
    SCREEN: Feel tension by looking

## [tip] heading: "Feel the Colors"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you see an illustration, ask: how does this make me feel?
    SCREEN: Ask: How does it feel?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to read the mood in pictures!
