# L.2.5 — Word Connections  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Words Connect"
  AUDIO: Words connect to each other in all kinds of ways.
    SCREEN: Words connect
  AUDIO: Sometimes words are alike, like 'happy' and 'joyful'.
    SCREEN: Words are alike
  AUDIO: Other times, words are opposites, like 'up' and 'down'.
    SCREEN: Words are opposites

## [teach] heading: "Synonyms and Antonyms"
  AUDIO: Synonyms mean almost the same. Happy and glad.
    SCREEN: Synonyms = same
  AUDIO: Antonyms are opposites. Hot and cold.
    SCREEN: Antonyms = opposites

## [teach] heading: "Groups of Words"
  AUDIO: Some words go together because they are in the same group.
    SCREEN: Words in same group
  AUDIO: Dog, cat, fish. All animals!
    SCREEN: Dog / Cat / Fish

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here are two words.
  AUDIO: The two words are big and huge.
    SCREEN: big and huge
  AUDIO: Do they mean the same or the opposite? The same!
    SCREEN: Same or opposite? / Same!
  AUDIO: So what do we call them? Synonyms!
    SCREEN: So they are? / Synonyms!
  AUDIO: Big and huge are synonyms because they mean almost the same. You made the connection!

## [tip] heading: "Make Connections"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you meet a new word, think: what words is this similar to?
    SCREEN: Words similar to?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to make word connections!
