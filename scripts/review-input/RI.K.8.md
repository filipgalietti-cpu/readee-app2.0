# RI.K.8 — Reasons and Support  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Learn About Reasons!"
  AUDIO: Sometimes an author tells us something and then explains WHY. Those are called reasons!
    SCREEN: Reasons explain WHY
  AUDIO: Reasons help us understand why the author thinks something is true.
    SCREEN: Reasons help us understand
  AUDIO: Let's learn how to find reasons in what we read!

## [teach] heading: "What Are Reasons?"
  AUDIO: An author might say: Dogs make great pets. But why? They need to give us reasons!
    SCREEN: Why? / Need reasons!
  AUDIO: Reasons are the facts that back up what the author says.
    SCREEN: Reasons = facts that back up
  AUDIO: Like: Dogs are loyal. Dogs are fun to play with. Those are reasons why dogs are great pets!
    SCREEN: Dogs are loyal

## [example] heading: "Let's Find the Reasons!"
  AUDIO: Listen to this and find the reasons!
  AUDIO: Fruit is good for you. It gives you energy. It has vitamins. It tastes yummy!
    SCREEN: Fruit is good for you. It gives you energy. It has vitamins. It tastes yummy!
  AUDIO: The big idea is 'fruit is good for you.' And the reasons are energy, vitamins, and yummy taste!
    SCREEN: Reasons

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When an author says something, look for the word 'because' or look for examples.
    SCREEN: Look for 'because' or examples
  AUDIO: Those are usually the reasons! They explain why the author's point makes sense.
    SCREEN: Those are the reasons
  AUDIO: You're a reason finder now!
