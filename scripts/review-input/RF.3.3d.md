## [teach] heading: "What Is a Silent Letter?"
  AUDIO: Look at the word knee. We do not say the K. We just say nee.
    SCREEN: knee = nee
  AUDIO: The K is written, but it stays silent. Your mouth skips right over it.
    SCREEN: K is silent

## [teach] heading: "Find the Quiet One"
  AUDIO: In the word comb, the B is silent. We say kome, not komb.
    SCREEN: comb = kome
  AUDIO: To find a silent letter, say the word slowly and listen for a letter you do not hear.
    SCREEN: Say it slow, listen

  AUDIO: So write starts with a silent W. You found the quiet letter!
