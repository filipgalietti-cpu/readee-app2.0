## [teach] heading: "Similes and Metaphors"
  AUDIO: Simile uses like or as. He ran like a cheetah.
    SCREEN: Simile: like or as
  AUDIO: Metaphor says something is something else. Her smile is sunshine.
    SCREEN: Metaphor: is something else

## [teach] heading: "Idioms"
  AUDIO: Idioms have hidden meanings. Piece of cake means easy!
    SCREEN: Idioms have hidden meanings

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here is a sentence.
  AUDIO: The brave knight was as quiet as a mouse.
    SCREEN: The brave knight was as quiet as a mouse.
  AUDIO: What type of figurative language is this? A simile! It uses the word as to compare.
    SCREEN: What type is it? / A simile!
  AUDIO: So what does it really mean? Very quiet! A mouse is super quiet, so the knight was too.
    SCREEN: What does it mean? / Very quiet.
  AUDIO: It is a simile that means very quiet. You found the hidden meaning!

  AUDIO: When words sound weird literally, look for a hidden meaning!
