# RI.1.3 — Connecting Ideas in Facts  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Ideas Connect"
  AUDIO: Facts in a book often connect to each other.
    SCREEN: Facts connect
  AUDIO: One thing happens. Then another thing happens because of it.
    SCREEN: One causes another
  AUDIO: Let us learn how to connect the dots!

## [teach] heading: "Cause and Effect"
  AUDIO: Listen. It rained all day. The next morning, puddles were everywhere.
    SCREEN: Rain, then puddles
  AUDIO: The rain caused the puddles. That is called cause and effect.
    SCREEN: Cause and effect
  AUDIO: The cause is what happened first. The effect is what happened next.
    SCREEN: Cause: first, Effect: next

## [example] heading: "Seeds and Sun"
  AUDIO: Seeds need water and sunlight to grow.
    SCREEN: Seeds need water + sun
  AUDIO: A farmer waters the seeds every day.
    SCREEN: Farmer waters daily
  AUDIO: Soon, small plants appear!
    SCREEN: Plants appear!
  AUDIO: What caused the plants? The water and sun! See the connection?
    SCREEN: What caused plants? / Water and sun!

## [tip] heading: "Ask Why"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Ask, why did this happen? The answer is usually the cause.
    SCREEN: Ask why: cause
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to connect ideas in fact books!
