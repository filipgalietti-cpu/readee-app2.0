## [teach] heading: "Cozy"
  AUDIO: The word cozy means warm and comfortable.
    SCREEN: Cozy: warm, comfortable
  AUDIO: What is cozy in real life?
    SCREEN: Cozy in real life?
  AUDIO: A soft blanket. A warm fireplace. A snuggly bed.
    SCREEN: Cozy examples

## [example] heading: "Let Us Try One"
  AUDIO: Let us try one together. Here is a word.
  AUDIO: Here is the word: soggy. Soggy means wet and droopy.
    SCREEN: soggy
  AUDIO: What is soggy in real life? A rainy day puddle!
    SCREEN: What is soggy? / A rainy day puddle!
  AUDIO: What else is like it? Cereal left too long in milk gets soggy.
    SCREEN: Anything else? / Cereal left in milk.
  AUDIO: So a wet puddle is soggy. You matched the word to real life!

## [example] heading: "Chilly"
  AUDIO: Chilly means a little bit cold.
    SCREEN: Chilly = a little cold
  AUDIO: What feels chilly?
    SCREEN: What feels chilly?
  AUDIO: Early morning air. A cool breeze. An ice cube!
    SCREEN: Early morning air

## [tip] heading: "Picture It"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you meet a new word, picture a real thing that matches it!
    SCREEN: Picture a real thing
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to connect words to real life!
