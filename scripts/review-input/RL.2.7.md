# RL.2.7 — Pictures Tell More  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Pictures Add to Stories"
  AUDIO: Pictures are part of the story too.
    SCREEN: Pictures are story
  AUDIO: They show details the words might not say.
    SCREEN: Show details words don't
  AUDIO: Let us use them together!

## [teach] heading: "See the Setting"
  AUDIO: The words might say: it was cold.
    SCREEN: it was cold.
  AUDIO: The picture shows snow, bare trees, and a deer in a coat.
    SCREEN: Picture: snow / Trees / Deer
  AUDIO: Now we know it is winter!
    SCREEN: It is winter!

## [teach] heading: "See the Feelings"
  AUDIO: The picture shows how the character feels.
    SCREEN: Picture shows feelings
  AUDIO: A huge smile means excited or proud.
    SCREEN: Smile: excited or proud
  AUDIO: Crossed arms and a frown? Maybe angry or upset.
    SCREEN: Frown, arms: angry/upset

## [tip] heading: "Pause and Look"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you read, pause on each picture. Ask, what does it add?
    SCREEN: Pause, ask what adds
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to use pictures to understand stories!
