# RI.3.6 — Author's Point of View  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Whose View Is It?"
  AUDIO: Every author has a point of view.
    SCREEN: Author's point of view
  AUDIO: You have your own view too. Notice when you agree or disagree.
    SCREEN: Your view: agree/disagree

## [teach] heading: "Look for Opinion Words"
  AUDIO: Words like best, most important, or should signal opinion.
    SCREEN: Opinion words signal
  AUDIO: Those are the author's view, not your view!
    SCREEN: Author's view, not yours

## [example] heading: "Your View vs. The Author's View"
  AUDIO: When we read, we first think about what the author believes or feels about the topic.
    SCREEN: Author's view first
  AUDIO: Then, we think about what we believe or feel about the same topic.
    SCREEN: Your view next
  AUDIO: Finally, we compare to see if our ideas are similar to the author's or if they are different.
    SCREEN: Compare views

## [tip] heading: "Highlight Opinions"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Circle opinion words as you read. That is where the author shows their view.
    SCREEN: Circle opinion words
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to spot author's view!
