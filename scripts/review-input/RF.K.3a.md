## [teach] heading: "Each Letter Has a Sound"
  AUDIO: Look at the letter ess. It hisses like a snake.
  AUDIO: The letter em. Like something yummy.
  AUDIO: The letter tee. Like a ticking clock.
  AUDIO: Every letter has its own sound. Learn the sounds and you unlock reading!

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together! I'll show you a letter, and we'll say the sound it makes.
  AUDIO: Here is our letter: P. Look at it carefully.
    SCREEN: P
  AUDIO: What is this letter's name? It is the letter Pee!
    SCREEN: Letter name? / Pee!
  AUDIO: And what sound does it make? Puh! Like the start of pop and pig.
    SCREEN: What sound? / Puh!
  AUDIO: So the letter P says Puh. You unlocked another letter sound. Way to go!

## [example] heading: "Let's Practice Some Sounds!"
  AUDIO: I'll show you a letter. You say its sound!
  AUDIO: Look at the letter bee. What sound does bee make?
  AUDIO: Now the letters dee and eff. Listen to each sound.
