# RF.K.2d — Sounds in Words  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Find Sounds in Words!"
  AUDIO: Words are made of sounds! And we can break them apart to hear each sound!
  AUDIO: Like the word 'cat.' Cat has three sounds.
  AUDIO: Let's learn to hear all the sounds hiding inside words!

## [teach] heading: "Breaking Words Into Sounds"
  AUDIO: Every word is made of individual sounds. We call them phonemes!
  AUDIO: The word 'dog' has three sounds.
  AUDIO: The word 'go' has two sounds.
  AUDIO: The trick is to say the word super slowly and count each sound!

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together! I'll say a word super slowly, and we'll count every sound.
  AUDIO: Here is our word: fish. Let's stretch it out. Ffff-iii-shhh.
    SCREEN: fish
  AUDIO: Say it slowly with me. Ffff-iii-shhh. We hold up a finger for each sound we hear.
    SCREEN: Stretch it out? / Ffff-iii-shhh!
  AUDIO: Now count it. Fff is one, iii is two, shhh is three. How many sounds? Three!
    SCREEN: How many sounds? / Three!
  AUDIO: So fish has three sounds. You counted every single one. You're a sound detective!

## [example] heading: "Let's Count Sounds Together!"
  AUDIO: I'll say a word slowly. Count the sounds with me!
  AUDIO: Let's try 'map.' Map has three sounds.
  AUDIO: Now try 'it.' It has two sounds.

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Use your fingers! Hold up one finger for each sound you hear.
  AUDIO: Say 'cat' slowly. Raise a finger for each sound you hear.
  AUDIO: You're a sound detective now!
