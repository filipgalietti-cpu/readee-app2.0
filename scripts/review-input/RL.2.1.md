# RL.2.1 — Story Detective Questions  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Dig Into Stories"
  AUDIO: Great readers dig for clues in every story.
    SCREEN: Readers dig clues
  AUDIO: We ask smart questions and hunt for the answers.
    SCREEN: Ask questions
  AUDIO: Let us learn what to ask!

## [teach] heading: "The Big Six"
  AUDIO: Detectives ask six big questions.
    SCREEN: Six big questions
  AUDIO: Who. What. Where. When.
    SCREEN: Who / What / Where / When
  AUDIO: And the hardest two. Why. And how!
    SCREEN: Why, How

## [example] heading: "Try It On a Story"
  AUDIO: Listen. Max the dog saw a red ball in the yard. He ran to fetch it.
    SCREEN: Max the dog saw a red ball in the yard. He ran to fetch it.
  AUDIO: Who? Max the dog!
    SCREEN: Who? / Max the dog!
  AUDIO: What did he do? He ran to fetch the ball.
    SCREEN: What? / Ran to fetch ball.
  AUDIO: Where? In the yard! Answers right in the words.
    SCREEN: Where? / In the yard!

## [tip] heading: "Find It in the Words"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: If you are not sure, look back at the words. The answer is usually hiding there.
    SCREEN: Look back at words.
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to ask story detective questions!
