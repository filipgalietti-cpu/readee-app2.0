# RL.3.1 — Asking Smart Story Questions  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Prove It With the Text"
  AUDIO: Third-grade readers do more than answer questions.
    SCREEN: Readers do more
  AUDIO: They prove their answers with quotes from the text.
    SCREEN: Prove with text

## [teach] heading: "Ask, Answer, Prove"
  AUDIO: Ask a question.
    SCREEN: Ask a question
  AUDIO: Find the answer.
    SCREEN: Find the answer
  AUDIO: Then point to the exact words that prove it.
    SCREEN: Prove with words

## [example] heading: "Try One"
  AUDIO: Question: how did Maya feel? Answer: proud. Proof: the text said she beamed with pride.
    SCREEN: How did Maya feel? / Proud.

## [tip] heading: "Point to the Words"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you answer, literally point back to the words that show it!
    SCREEN: Point to the words
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to ask smart story questions!
