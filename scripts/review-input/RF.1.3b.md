# RF.1.3b — Sounding Out Short Words  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Sound It Out"
  AUDIO: Short words are made of just a few sounds.
    SCREEN: Few sounds
  AUDIO: When you sound them out, you read each one in order.
    SCREEN: One at a time
  AUDIO: Then you blend them together. That is reading!
    SCREEN: Blend = reading

## [teach] heading: "Read CAT"
  AUDIO: Let us read this word.
    SCREEN: Read this
  AUDIO: First sound.
  AUDIO: Middle sound.
  AUDIO: Last sound.
  AUDIO: Now blend. Cat!

## [example] heading: "Your Turn: DOG"
  AUDIO: Your turn.
  AUDIO: First.
  AUDIO: Middle.
  AUDIO: Last.
  AUDIO: Blend! Dog!

## [tip] heading: "Stretch, Then Snap"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
    SCREEN: A decoding trick
  AUDIO: Say each sound slowly. Then say them faster and faster until they snap together!
    SCREEN: Slow, then snap
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to sound out short words!
    SCREEN: You got it!
