# RF.1.3a — Two-Letter Sounds  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Two Letters, One Sound"
  AUDIO: Sometimes two letters team up to make just one sound!
  AUDIO: The letters are not each saying their own sound anymore.
    SCREEN: Not by themselves
  AUDIO: They make a brand new sound together!
    SCREEN: A new sound!

## [teach] heading: "Meet SH"
  AUDIO: When S and H team up, they make the "shhh" sound.
    SCREEN: S and H: shhh sound
  AUDIO: Like in the word ship. Sh-ip.
    SCREEN: ship
  AUDIO: Or shell, shop, and shoe. All start with the "shhh" sound.
    SCREEN: Or shell / Shop / Shoe

## [teach] heading: "Meet CH and TH"
  AUDIO: C and H together make the "chh" sound. Like in chip.
    SCREEN: CH makes 'chh' sound
  AUDIO: Chip. Chop. Cheese. Hear the "chh"?
    SCREEN: Chip
  AUDIO: And T and H make the "thh" sound. Like in thumb.
    SCREEN: TH makes 'thh' sound
  AUDIO: Thumb. Thin. Think. Feel your tongue?
    SCREEN: Thumb

## [tip] heading: "Listen for Team Sounds"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you see S-H, C-H, or T-H together, they make one sound, not two.
    SCREEN: One sound, not two
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to spot two-letter sounds!
