# L.1.4 — Word Meaning Detective  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "One Word, Two Meanings"
  AUDIO: Some words have more than one meaning!
    SCREEN: More than one meaning
  AUDIO: The word bat can be a baseball bat, or a little flying animal.
    SCREEN: Bat: baseball or animal
  AUDIO: Let us learn how to tell which meaning fits.

## [teach] heading: "The Sentence Helps"
  AUDIO: Read the whole sentence. It gives you a clue.
    SCREEN: Read whole sentence
  AUDIO: The boy swung the bat at the ball.
    SCREEN: The boy swung the bat at the ball.
  AUDIO: Which bat? The baseball bat!
    SCREEN: Baseball bat

## [teach] heading: "Try It and See"
  AUDIO: Let me show you a detective trick. Here is the sentence: Park your bike here. The word park could mean a green park with grass, or it could mean to stop and leave something.
    SCREEN: Park your bike here.
  AUDIO: So I try one meaning and ask, does it make sense? Stop and leave your bike here. Yes! That makes sense. The green-grass park does not fit. Trying the meaning shows me which one is right.
    SCREEN: Does it make sense?

## [example] heading: "Try Another"
  AUDIO: Listen. The bat flew out of the cave at night.
    SCREEN: The bat flew out of the cave at night.
  AUDIO: Did a baseball bat fly? No!
    SCREEN: Baseball bat? / No!
  AUDIO: This bat is the flying animal. The sentence gave us the clue.
    SCREEN: Sentence gave clue

## [tip] heading: "Be a Word Detective"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When a word could mean two things, read the whole sentence for clues.
    SCREEN: Read sentence for clues
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to be a word meaning detective!
