# RF.1.3e — Two-Syllable Words  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Bigger Words, Two Beats"
  AUDIO: Some words are long! But long words can hide a secret. They split into two little pieces.
    SCREEN: Two pieces
  AUDIO: Each piece is called a syllable. Two pieces means two syllables.
    SCREEN: Each piece is a syllable
  AUDIO: If we split the word, read each piece, then blend it back, we can read any big word!
    SCREEN: Split, read, blend

## [teach] heading: "Split Between Twins"
  AUDIO: Here is the trick. When you see two of the same letter in the middle, split right between them. We call them twin letters.
    SCREEN: Split between twin letters
  AUDIO: Look at rabbit. It has twin b's in the middle. Split between them: rab and bit. Rabbit!
    SCREEN: rabbit splits to? / rab-bit

## [example] heading: "Let Us Try One"
  AUDIO: Let us try one together. Here is the word: happen.
    SCREEN: happen
  AUDIO: What are the twin letters in the middle? The two p's!
    SCREEN: Twin letters? / Two p's!
  AUDIO: Split right between the p's. Hap and pen.
    SCREEN: Split it? / hap-pen
  AUDIO: Read each piece. Hap. Pen. Now blend them. Happen!
    SCREEN: Blend it? / Happen!

## [tip] heading: "Look For The Twins"
  AUDIO: Here is a trick to remember. Find the twin letters first, then split right between them.
    SCREEN: Find the twins, split between
  AUDIO: Twin letters always tell you where to break the word in two!
    SCREEN: Twins show the break
