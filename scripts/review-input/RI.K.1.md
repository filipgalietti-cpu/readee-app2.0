# RI.K.1 — Key Details in Facts  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Find Key Details in Facts!"
  AUDIO: Hey! Did you know that some books teach us real facts about the world?
  AUDIO: These are called informational books, and they're full of amazing things to learn!
    SCREEN: Informational books = facts
  AUDIO: Just like storybooks, fact books have key details too. Let's find out how to spot them!
    SCREEN: Fact books: key details

## [teach] heading: "Key Details in Fact Books"
  AUDIO: When we read a fact book, key details are the most important pieces of information.
    SCREEN: Key details: important info
  AUDIO: They answer questions like: What is it?
    SCREEN: What is it?
  AUDIO: What does it do?
    SCREEN: What does it do?
  AUDIO: And where does it live? When you can answer questions like these, you've found the key details!
    SCREEN: Where does it live?

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Let me read you some facts, and we'll find the key details together.
  AUDIO: Penguins live in cold places. They eat fish. They cannot fly.
    SCREEN: Penguins live in cold places.
They eat fish.
They cannot fly.
  AUDIO: Now, where do penguins live? Cold places! That's a key detail!
    SCREEN: Where? / Cold places!
  AUDIO: What do they eat? Fish! Another key detail!
    SCREEN: What do they eat? / Fish!
  AUDIO: Can they fly? Nope! That's an important fact too. Look at you — you found all the key details!
    SCREEN: Can they fly? / No!

## [tip] heading: "Fun Facts About Otters!"
  AUDIO: Here are some fun facts about sea otters.
  AUDIO: They have the thickest fur of any animal.
    SCREEN: Thickest fur of animals
  AUDIO: When they sleep, they hold hands so they don't float away.
    SCREEN: Hold hands when sleeping
  AUDIO: They even use rocks to crack open their food.
    SCREEN: Use rocks to open food
