  AUDIO: There are some words you will see in almost every book.

## [teach] heading: "What Are Sight Words?"
  AUDIO: Sight words are words you learn to read right away, just by looking at them.
    SCREEN: Read right away
  AUDIO: You do not sound them out, you just know them! Like how you know your own name.
    SCREEN: Don't sound out
  AUDIO: The more sight words you know, the faster you can read! They are reading superpowers!
    SCREEN: Faster reading

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together! Here is a sight word we read just by looking.
  AUDIO: Here is our word: and. You will see it in almost every book.
    SCREEN: and
  AUDIO: Can you read it just by looking? It says and!
    SCREEN: What word? / And!
  AUDIO: Do we sound it out? No! It is a sight word, so we just know it, fast as lightning.
    SCREEN: Sound it out? / No, just know it!
  AUDIO: So and is a sight word you read right away. One more reading superpower. Nice work!

## [example] heading: "Let's Learn Some!"
  AUDIO: Here are some important sight words. Let us read them together!
  AUDIO: The. You see this word in almost every book.
  AUDIO: I. That is what you call yourself.
  AUDIO: Do. Like in, I do it!
  AUDIO: Is. Like in, he is here.
  AUDIO: The word you. I am talking to you right now!
  AUDIO: Those are five sight words you will see everywhere. Nice work!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Practice reading sight words every day, even just a few minutes!
  AUDIO: You can put them on flashcards, stick them on the fridge, or find them in your books!
    SCREEN: Practice every day!
  AUDIO: Soon you'll spot them everywhere, lightning fast!
