# RL.1.1 — Asking Story Questions  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Story Detectives"
  AUDIO: Great readers are like detectives. They look for clues in a story.
    SCREEN: Great readers are / detectives.
  AUDIO: They ask questions and hunt for answers in the words.
    SCREEN: They ask questions.
  AUDIO: Let us learn some detective questions to ask!
    SCREEN: Let's learn how!
  AUDIO: Story detectives ask Who, What, and Where.
    SCREEN: Who? / What? / Where?

## [teach] heading: "Story Questions"
  AUDIO: Good detectives ask five big questions.
  AUDIO: Who is the story about?
  AUDIO: What are they doing?
  AUDIO: Where are they? And when?
  AUDIO: And why did it happen?

## [example] heading: "Let Us Try One"
  AUDIO: Listen to this little story.
  AUDIO: Bella picked three red apples from the tree. She put them in her basket.
    SCREEN: Bella picked three red apples from the tree. She put them in her basket.
  AUDIO: Who is the story about? Bella!
    SCREEN: Who? / Bella!
  AUDIO: What did she do? She picked apples.
    SCREEN: What? / Picked apples.
  AUDIO: How many apples? Three. We found the answer in the words!
    SCREEN: How many? / Three!

## [tip] heading: "A Detective Trick"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
    SCREEN: A Detective Trick
  AUDIO: When you finish reading, go back and find the answer in the words.
    SCREEN: Find it in the words.
  AUDIO: You got it!
    SCREEN: You got it!

## [interactive] heading: "Your Turn, Detective!"
  FORK kind=tap Q(spoken): Your turn, detective! Listen closely. Sam ran to the pond. He saw two green frogs. How many frogs did Sam see?
  on-screen: How many frogs? | ['Two', 'Three', 'Five'] ✓Two
