## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a short fable.
  AUDIO: The slow turtle kept going. He beat the fast, lazy hare.
    SCREEN: The slow turtle kept going. He beat the fast, lazy hare.
  AUDIO: Who won the race? The slow turtle did!
    SCREEN: Who won? / The slow turtle.
  AUDIO: What's the lesson it teaches? Keep going and don't give up!
    SCREEN: What's the lesson? / Keep going!
  AUDIO: So the fable's message is: slow and steady wins. You found the lesson!

## [tip] heading: "What Did They Learn?"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: At the end, ask: what did the character learn? That is the message!
    SCREEN: Character learn = message
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to find lessons in folktales!
