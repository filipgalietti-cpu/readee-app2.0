# RI.2.1 — Asking Smart Fact Questions  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Smart Fact Questions"
  AUDIO: Smart readers ask questions as they read facts.
    SCREEN: Readers ask questions
  AUDIO: Six big ones: who, what, where, when, why, how.
    SCREEN: Who / What / Where / When / Why
  AUDIO: Every one has an answer in the text.
    SCREEN: Answers in the text

## [teach] heading: "Why and How Matter Most"
  AUDIO: Who, what, where, and when are easy to spot.
    SCREEN: Who / What / Where / When easy
  AUDIO: Why and how need deeper reading.
    SCREEN: Why, how: deeper reading
  AUDIO: They help you really understand the facts.
    SCREEN: Understand the facts

## [teach] heading: "Find the Answer"
  AUDIO: Every smart question has an answer hiding right in the words. Read closely to find it.
    SCREEN: Answer hides in words

## [example] heading: "Try It"
  AUDIO: Listen. Seal pups are born on ice. Their moms teach them to swim.
    SCREEN: Pups born on ice
  AUDIO: Who? The seal pups! Where? On ice!
    SCREEN: Who? → Seal pups!
  AUDIO: Who teaches them? Their moms. Why? So they can survive!
    SCREEN: Who teaches? → Moms

## [tip] heading: "Write Questions"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Jot questions on sticky notes as you read. Then hunt for the answers!
    SCREEN: Jot questions, hunt answers
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to ask smart fact questions!
