# RF.K.1b — Words Are Made of Letters  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Words Are Made of Letters!"
  AUDIO: Did you know that every word you see is made up of letters? Like building blocks!
    SCREEN: Words made of letters
  AUDIO: The letters come together to make a word. C, A, T makes cat!
    SCREEN: Letters make words
  AUDIO: Let's learn about how letters build words!

## [teach] heading: "Letters Build Words!"
  AUDIO: Letters are the tiny pieces that make up every word.
    SCREEN: Letters make words
  AUDIO: Some words are short — like 'at.' That's just two letters! A and T.
    SCREEN: Short words: 'at'
  AUDIO: Some words are longer — like 'dog.' That's three letters! D, O, G.
    SCREEN: Longer words: 'dog'
  AUDIO: Every word, short or long, is made of letters put together!
    SCREEN: Words made of letters

## [example] heading: "Let's Count the Letters!"
  AUDIO: Let's look at the word 'sun.' How many letters does it have?
  AUDIO: S... U... N! That's three letters. S, U, N makes sun!
    SCREEN: Sun: 3 letters
  AUDIO: Now try 'me.' M... E! Just two letters. Easy!
    SCREEN: Me: 2 letters

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Here's a fun trick. Think about your own name!
  AUDIO: How many letters are in YOUR name? Count them with your finger!
    SCREEN: Count name letters
  AUDIO: Every name is made of letters, just like every word!
    SCREEN: Words are letters
