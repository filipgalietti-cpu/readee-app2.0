# RI.1.8 — Reasons the Author Gives  (grade 1st Grade)

    SCREEN: Authors convince
  AUDIO: They make a point, then give us reasons why.

  AUDIO: Good readers spot the reasons!

  AUDIO: Listen. You should drink water every day.
    SCREEN: Drink water every day
  AUDIO: Water helps your body work well.
    SCREEN: Water helps body work
  AUDIO: And it keeps you from getting thirsty.

  AUDIO: Two reasons! That is how authors back up their point.
    SCREEN: Authors back up point

## [example] heading: "Great Pets"
  AUDIO: Dogs make great pets. Here is why.
  AUDIO: They are loyal.
    SCREEN: They are loyal
  AUDIO: They are playful.
    SCREEN: They are playful
  AUDIO: And they love their owners.
    SCREEN: Love their owners
  AUDIO: Three reasons that back up the point!
    SCREEN: Three reasons back point

## [tip] heading: "Count the Reasons"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When the author says because, for example, or first and second, those are reasons!
    SCREEN: because, for example = reasons
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to find author reasons!
