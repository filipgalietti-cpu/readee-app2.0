## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Listen for the details that prove the answer.
  AUDIO: Bats sleep all day in dark caves. They hunt for insects at night.
    SCREEN: Bats sleep all day in dark caves. They hunt for insects at night.
  AUDIO: When do bats hunt? At night. The text says so right there.
    SCREEN: When do bats hunt? / At night.
  AUDIO: How do you know? Because the text says they hunt at night. That's your evidence.
    SCREEN: How do you know? / The text says it.
  AUDIO: You backed your answer with text evidence. That's how detectives read nonfiction!
