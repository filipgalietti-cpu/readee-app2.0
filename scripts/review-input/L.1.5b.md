# L.1.5b — Defining Words  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "What Is a Dog?"
  AUDIO: A good definition tells two things.
    SCREEN: Definition tells two things
  AUDIO: What group is it in? And what is special about it?
    SCREEN: Group and special
  AUDIO: Let us try defining a dog.

## [teach] heading: "Group + Detail"
  AUDIO: A dog is an animal. That is its group.
    SCREEN: Dog's group: animal
  AUDIO: But not every animal is a dog.
  AUDIO: A dog is an animal that barks and wags its tail. That is special about dogs!
    SCREEN: Details: barks, wags tail

## [example] heading: "Define an Apple"
  AUDIO: An apple is a fruit. That is the group.
    SCREEN: Group: a fruit
  AUDIO: An apple is a fruit that is round and grows on a tree.
    SCREEN: Attributes: round, tree
  AUDIO: Now we have a clear definition!

## [tip] heading: "Two-Part Definition"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Give the group name, and one special thing. That is a great definition!
    SCREEN: Group name, special thing
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to define words like a pro!
