# RL.3.9 — Comparing Themes and Plots  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Two Stories, One Idea"
  AUDIO: Did you ever read two different stories that taught the same big lesson?
    SCREEN: Two stories can share an idea
  AUDIO: The big idea or lesson in a story is called the theme.
    SCREEN: Theme = big idea or lesson
  AUDIO: When we compare two stories, we look at how their themes and plots are alike and different.
    SCREEN: Compare = look at both

## [teach] heading: "Same Theme, Different Story"
  AUDIO: Two stories can have very different characters and places but still teach the same theme.
    SCREEN: Different stories, same theme
  AUDIO: A story about a turtle and a story about a runner can both teach: never give up.
    SCREEN: Both teach? / Never give up

## [teach] heading: "Compare Side by Side"
  AUDIO: To compare two stories, ask what each one teaches, then find the idea they share.
    SCREEN: Find the idea they share
  AUDIO: The plots can be totally different even when the theme is exactly the same.
    SCREEN: Plots differ, theme matches

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here are two short stories.
    SCREEN: Two stories to compare
  AUDIO: Story one: Ant carried a crumb up the hill. It slipped down again and again, but Ant kept climbing until it reached the top.
    SCREEN: Ant kept climbing until it reached the top.
  AUDIO: Story two: Kira missed the basket many times. She practiced every day until she finally made the shot.
    SCREEN: Kira practiced until she made the shot.
  AUDIO: What does each story teach? Both characters keep trying after they fail.
    SCREEN: Both characters? / Keep trying
  AUDIO: So the theme both stories share is perseverance. Two different plots, one big idea!
    SCREEN: Shared theme? / Perseverance

## [tip] heading: "Find the Shared Idea"
  AUDIO: Here is a trick. Finish this sentence for each story: this story teaches me to blank.
    SCREEN: This story teaches me to ___
  AUDIO: If both blanks match, you found the theme the two stories share!
    SCREEN: Both match = shared theme
