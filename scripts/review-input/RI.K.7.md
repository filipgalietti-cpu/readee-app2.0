# RI.K.7 — Pictures Help Us Learn  (grade Kindergarten)

    SCREEN: Pictures are helpful
  AUDIO: Pictures, photos, and diagrams can teach us things that words alone can't show!
    SCREEN: Pictures teach what words can't
  AUDIO: Let's learn how to use pictures to understand facts even better!

## [teach] heading: "How Do Pictures Help?"
  AUDIO: Pictures help us understand fact books in two special ways!
    SCREEN: Pictures help us understand
  AUDIO: When a fact book says elephants are ENORMOUS, the picture shows you just HOW big they really are!
    SCREEN: Show what things look like
  AUDIO: Pictures can also show us steps in order, or parts of something. They're like visual helpers!
    SCREEN: Show steps or parts

## [example] heading: "Let's Look at a Picture!"
  AUDIO: Here's a picture from a fact book. What can it teach us?
  AUDIO: Look! This picture shows how a frog grows. First an egg, then a tadpole, then a froglet, then a frog!
    SCREEN: egg → tadpole → froglet → frog
  AUDIO: The picture makes it so easy to understand! Way easier than just reading words about it.
    SCREEN: Picture makes it easy

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When you read a fact book, ask yourself two questions about every picture!
    SCREEN: Ask two questions
  AUDIO: First ask: What do I see? That shows you what things LOOK like!
    SCREEN: What do I see? → LOOK
  AUDIO: Then ask: What is it teaching me? That shows you what the words MEAN!
    SCREEN: What teaching? → MEAN
  AUDIO: Those two questions help you learn from every picture!
    SCREEN: Two questions help
