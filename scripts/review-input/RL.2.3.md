# RL.2.3 — How Characters React  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Characters Have Feelings"
  AUDIO: When things happen in a story, characters react.
    SCREEN: Characters react
  AUDIO: They might feel happy, scared, angry, or proud.
    SCREEN: Happy / Scared / Angry / Proud
  AUDIO: How a character reacts tells us who they are.
    SCREEN: Reactions show who they are

## [teach] heading: "Listen to Their Reaction"
  AUDIO: Sam broke his favorite toy. He was heartbroken.
    SCREEN: Sam: heartbroken
  AUDIO: Heartbroken shows how deep his feeling was.
    SCREEN: Heartbroken shows feeling
  AUDIO: Then his friend fixed it. He was overjoyed!
    SCREEN: Fixed → overjoyed

## [example] heading: "When Problems Come"
  AUDIO: Big problems show how characters handle tough moments.
    SCREEN: Problems show character
  AUDIO: Maya had a huge pile of homework. She did not give up.
    SCREEN: Maya didn't give up
  AUDIO: She is the kind of character who stays brave!
    SCREEN: Stays brave

## [tip] heading: "Watch the Reactions"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When something happens, watch what the character does next. That is their reaction!
    SCREEN: Reaction: what happens
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to describe how characters react!
