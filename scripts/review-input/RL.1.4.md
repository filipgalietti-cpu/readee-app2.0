# RL.1.4 — Words That Show Feelings  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Feeling Words"
  AUDIO: Writers choose special words to make us feel something.
    SCREEN: Words make us feel
  AUDIO: Some words help us hear, see, or touch a story in our mind.
    SCREEN: Words appeal to senses
  AUDIO: Let us find some feeling words!

## [teach] heading: "Words That Show Feelings"
  AUDIO: Today, we will learn about words that tell us how characters feel.
  AUDIO: Words like happy, excited, or joyful show us good feelings.
    SCREEN: Good feelings
  AUDIO: Other words like sad, angry, or scared tell us when characters have different feelings.
    SCREEN: Other feelings
  AUDIO: These feeling words help us understand what is happening in a story.
    SCREEN: Feeling words help understand

## [teach] heading: "The Word Does the Work"
  AUDIO: Watch how one word changes everything. Here is a plain sentence: She walked to the door.
    SCREEN: She walked to the door.
  AUDIO: Now I will swap in a feeling word. She stomped to the door. Stomped makes me feel that she is angry! The very same girl, but one word tells me how she feels inside.
    SCREEN: stomped

## [example] heading: "Warm Sun"
  AUDIO: Listen. The bright sun warmed her face.
    SCREEN: The bright sun warmed her face.
  AUDIO: Which word helps us feel the sun? Warmed!
    SCREEN: Which word? / Warmed!
  AUDIO: That word makes us feel the sun on our own skin.
    SCREEN: Feel sun on skin

## [tip] heading: "Read It and Feel It"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you read a feeling word, stop and feel it for a moment.
    SCREEN: Feeling word / Stop and / Feel it
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to practice finding feeling words!
