## [teach] heading: "Stop, Think, Try Again"
  AUDIO: Step one: stop when it feels off.
    SCREEN: Stop when it feels off
  AUDIO: Step two: think. Does that word fit?
    SCREEN: Think: Does that word fit?
  AUDIO: Step three: try again with the right word.
    SCREEN: Try again with right word

## [example] heading: "The Cat Sat"
  AUDIO: You read: the cat sat on the rug. But you said map.
    SCREEN: the cat sat on the rug.
  AUDIO: Does a cat sit on a map? No! Stop, think, try again.
    SCREEN: Cat sit on map? / No!
  AUDIO: Rug! That makes sense.
    SCREEN: Rug makes sense.

## [tip] heading: "Ask If It Makes Sense"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: After each sentence, ask: did that make sense?
    SCREEN: Ask: Did it make sense?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to self-correct while reading!
