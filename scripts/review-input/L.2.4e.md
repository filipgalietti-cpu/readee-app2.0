## [intro] heading: "Glossaries Help"
  AUDIO: A glossary is like a mini dictionary at the back of a book.
    SCREEN: Glossary: mini dictionary
  AUDIO: It defines the important words used in the book.

## [teach] heading: "Alphabetical Order"
  AUDIO: Glossaries are in ABC order.
    SCREEN: Glossaries in ABC order
  AUDIO: So you can find a word fast. Just use the alphabet!
    SCREEN: Find word fast

## [teach] heading: "Look and Learn"
  AUDIO: See a bold word in the book? Flip to the glossary!
    SCREEN: Flip to glossary
  AUDIO: You will find the meaning right there.
    SCREEN: Find the meaning

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. You see a bold word in your book.
  AUDIO: The bold word is habitat. Let's use the glossary.
    SCREEN: habitat
  AUDIO: Where do you look? Flip to the glossary!
    SCREEN: Where do you look? / The glossary!
  AUDIO: Habitat starts with H, so we find it near the start. What letter do we hunt for? H!
    SCREEN: What letter first? / H!
  AUDIO: The glossary tells us a habitat is an animal's home. You found the meaning!

## [tip] heading: "Use It Often"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Put a bookmark at the glossary so you can flip there fast!
    SCREEN: Bookmark the glossary
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to use a glossary!
