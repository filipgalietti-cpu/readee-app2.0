# RF.K.4 — Reading with Purpose  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Read with Purpose!"
  AUDIO: When we read, it helps to know WHY we're reading. Are we reading for fun? To learn something?
    SCREEN: Why read?
  AUDIO: Reading with purpose means knowing what we're looking for. It makes reading even better!
    SCREEN: Reading with purpose
  AUDIO: Let's learn how to read with purpose!

## [teach] heading: "Why Are We Reading?"
  AUDIO: There are two big reasons we read a book!
  AUDIO: Number one. A storybook. We read it for fun!
    SCREEN: Storybook: for fun
  AUDIO: Number two. A fact book. We read it to learn!
    SCREEN: Fact book: to learn

## [example] heading: "Let's Try It!"
  AUDIO: Imagine you pick up a book about tigers. Why would you read it?
  AUDIO: Our purpose is to find out where tigers live. That is what we are looking for as we read.
    SCREEN: Purpose: find where tigers live
  AUDIO: And here is the answer! Tigers live in forests and jungles. We found what we were looking for!
    SCREEN: Where? / Forests and jungles!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Before you read, ask yourself one question about the book.
    SCREEN: Ask one question
  AUDIO: Then look for the answer while you read! That gives you a mission!
    SCREEN: Look for the answer
  AUDIO: Reading with a mission is way more fun!
    SCREEN: Reading is fun!
