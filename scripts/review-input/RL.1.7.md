# RL.1.7 — Pictures and Stories Together  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Pictures Tell Stories Too"
  AUDIO: Pictures in a book are not just decoration.
    SCREEN: Not just decoration
  AUDIO: They give us clues about characters, places, and feelings!
    SCREEN: Clues
  AUDIO: Let us learn what to look for.

## [teach] heading: "A Happy Face Tells a Lot"
  AUDIO: Look at this picture.
  AUDIO: A girl with a big smile, holding a trophy.
    SCREEN: A girl with a big smile, holding a trophy.
  AUDIO: How does she probably feel? Happy! Proud!
    SCREEN: Happy! Proud!
  AUDIO: The picture told us. We did not even need words.
    SCREEN: No words needed!

## [teach] heading: "Pictures Show the Setting"
  AUDIO: Pictures also show us where and when.
    SCREEN: Pictures show where and when
  AUDIO: Snow on the ground. Kids in coats and hats. What season is it?
    SCREEN: Snow on the ground. Kids in coats and hats.
  AUDIO: Winter! The picture showed us.

## [tip] heading: "Look and Read Together"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Look at the picture and read the words. Both tell the story together.
    SCREEN: Look + read
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to practice using pictures as clues!
