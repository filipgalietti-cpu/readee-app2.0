## [teach] heading: "Quote the Text"
  AUDIO: When you make a claim, quote the exact words from the text.
    SCREEN: Quote exact words
  AUDIO: This helps you show exactly where your information comes from and makes your claims stronger.
    SCREEN: Strengthens claims
  AUDIO: Remember to always use quotation marks around the words you take directly from the text.
    SCREEN: Use quotation marks

## [example] heading: "Make Inferences"
  AUDIO: Sometimes the answer is not stated directly.
    SCREEN: Answer not direct
  AUDIO: We infer it from the details.
    SCREEN: Infer from details
  AUDIO: To infer means to put clues together.
    SCREEN: Infer: put clues together

## [tip] heading: "Always Cite"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you answer, say: the text says... and quote a line!
    SCREEN: Cite the text
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to find story details!
