## [teach] heading: "How Does It Work?"
  AUDIO: Words are made of sounds. If you swap one sound, the whole word changes!
  AUDIO: You can change the beginning sound. 'Mat' becomes 'sat' when M changes to S.
  AUDIO: Change the ending sound too. 'Cat' becomes 'can' when T changes to N.
  AUDIO: One little change, one brand new word!

## [example] heading: "Let's Make New Words!"
  AUDIO: Let's play the sound-swapping game!
  AUDIO: Start with 'hop.' Change the H to M. What new word do you get? Mop!
  AUDIO: Start with 'big.' Change the B to D. What new word do you get? Dig!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When you swap a sound, say the new word out loud!
  AUDIO: Try it. Start with 'top.' Change the T to a P and you get 'pop!'
  AUDIO: You're a word-changing wizard!
