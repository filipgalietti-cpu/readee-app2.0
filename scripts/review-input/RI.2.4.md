# RI.2.4 — Word Meanings in Texts  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "New Words, No Fear"
  AUDIO: Fact books sometimes use fancy words.
    SCREEN: Fact books: fancy words
  AUDIO: But the text gives us clues to their meaning.
    SCREEN: Text gives clues
  AUDIO: Let us learn to find them!

## [teach] heading: "Definitions Right There"
  AUDIO: Sometimes the text just tells you what a word means.
    SCREEN: Text tells meaning
  AUDIO: A habitat is the place where an animal lives.
    SCREEN: Habitat: place animal lives
  AUDIO: Those definitions are gold!

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together, word detective. Here's a sentence from a fact book.
  AUDIO: The famished puppy ate every last bite of food.
    SCREEN: Famished puppy ate everything
  AUDIO: What clue do the other words give? The puppy ate every last bite.
    SCREEN: Clue: ate every bite
  AUDIO: So what does it mean? Famished must mean very hungry!
    SCREEN: So what's famished? / Very hungry!
  AUDIO: The clue told us famished means very hungry. Great detective work!

## [example] heading: "Context Is Your Friend"
  AUDIO: Other times you have to use the words around it.
    SCREEN: Use words around it
  AUDIO: The thirsty camel gulped a lot of water.
    SCREEN: Thirsty camel gulped water
  AUDIO: Gulp must mean drink fast!
    SCREEN: Gulp = drink fast
  AUDIO: The thirst gave the clue.
    SCREEN: Thirst gave the clue

## [tip] heading: "Never Skip"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Do not skip hard words. Slow down and look for clues.
    SCREEN: Look for clues
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to figure out word meanings!
