# RL.3.3 — What Characters Are Like  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Characters Drive Stories"
  AUDIO: Characters' actions shape what happens in the story.
    SCREEN: Actions shape the story
  AUDIO: Their traits, feelings, and choices push the plot forward.
    SCREEN: Traits / Feelings / Choices

## [teach] heading: "Traits Are Who They Are"
  AUDIO: Character traits are their personality. Brave, kind, curious, selfish.
    SCREEN: Traits = personality
  AUDIO: We find traits by watching what they do and say.
    SCREEN: Find traits: do + say

## [example] heading: "Brave Nia"
  AUDIO: Nia climbed a tall ladder to save a kitten.
    SCREEN: Nia climbed a tall ladder to save a kitten.
  AUDIO: That action tells us she is brave and caring!
    SCREEN: Brave and caring

## [tip] heading: "Watch Their Choices"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: What does the character choose to do? Their choices reveal who they are.
    SCREEN: Choices reveal character
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to describe characters!
