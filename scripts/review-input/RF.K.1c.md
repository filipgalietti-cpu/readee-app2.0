# RF.K.1c — Spaces Between Words  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Spaces Between Words!"
  AUDIO: When we write, we put little spaces between each word. Do you know why?
    SCREEN: Spaces between words
  AUDIO: Spaces help us see where one word ends and the next word begins!
    SCREEN: Spaces separate words
  AUDIO: Without spaces, everything would be all mushed together! Let's learn more!
    SCREEN: No spaces: mushed words

## [teach] heading: "Why Do We Need Spaces?"
  AUDIO: Look at this: thecatsat. Can you read that? It's so hard!
    SCREEN: Can you read? / It's hard!
  AUDIO: Now look: the cat sat. So much easier! The spaces tell us each word.
    SCREEN: Spaces tell us words
  AUDIO: Every word needs a space before the next word starts. One finger space!
    SCREEN: One finger space!

## [example] heading: "Let's Find the Words!"
  AUDIO: Look at this sentence. Can you count how many words there are? Look for the spaces!
  AUDIO: I... like... dogs. I see two spaces, so there are three words!
    SCREEN: Two spaces, three words
  AUDIO: The spaces showed us exactly where each word is. Nice counting!
    SCREEN: Spaces show words

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When you write, put a finger space between each word!
    SCREEN: Finger space between words
  AUDIO: Put your finger down after writing a word, then start the next word after your finger. Perfect spaces every time!
    SCREEN: Finger after each word
  AUDIO: You're a spacing superstar!

## [interactive] heading: "Count the Words!"
  FORK kind=tap Q(spoken): Your turn! Look at the sentence. Count the spaces to find each word. How many words do you see?
