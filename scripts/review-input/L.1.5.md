## [example] heading: "Let Us Try One"

  AUDIO: Here are the words: up and down.
    SCREEN: up and down
  AUDIO: Are these the same, or opposite? Opposite!
    SCREEN: Same or opposite? / Opposite!
  AUDIO: Why are they opposite? Up and down go very different ways.
    SCREEN: Why opposite? / They go different ways.
  AUDIO: So up and down are opposites. You connected the words!
