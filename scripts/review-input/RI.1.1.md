# RI.1.1 — Asking Fact Questions  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Fact Finders"
  AUDIO: Fact books are full of real information.
    SCREEN: Fact books: real information
  AUDIO: Great readers ask questions and find the answers in the words.
    SCREEN: Readers ask questions
  AUDIO: Let us learn to be fact finders!
    SCREEN: Learn to find facts

## [teach] heading: "Five Big Questions"
  AUDIO: Fact finders ask five big questions.
  AUDIO: Who is it about?
    SCREEN: Who?
  AUDIO: What is happening?
    SCREEN: What?
  AUDIO: Where? And when?
    SCREEN: Where? When?
  AUDIO: And why does it happen?
    SCREEN: Why?

## [example] heading: "Penguins Live Where?"
  AUDIO: Listen to this fact.
  AUDIO: Penguins live in very cold places. They cannot fly, but they are great swimmers.
    SCREEN: Penguins live in very cold places. They cannot fly, but they are great swimmers.
  AUDIO: Where do penguins live? Very cold places!
    SCREEN: Where? / Very cold places!
  AUDIO: Can penguins fly? No. But they swim great!
    SCREEN: Can fly? / No. Swim great!

## [tip] heading: "Question Words Help Us Ask!"
  AUDIO: We can use special question words to help us ask about key details!
  AUDIO: The word 'Who' helps us ask about people or characters.
    SCREEN: Who: people or characters
  AUDIO: The word 'What' helps us ask about things or actions.
    SCREEN: What: things or actions
  AUDIO: The word 'Where' helps us ask about places.
    SCREEN: Where: places
  AUDIO: Now you know how to ask great fact questions!
