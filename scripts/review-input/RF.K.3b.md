## [intro] heading: "Let's Learn About Vowels!"
  AUDIO: There are five super special letters called vowels!
  AUDIO: First, the letter A.
  AUDIO: Next, E.
  AUDIO: Then, I.
  AUDIO: Now, O.
  AUDIO: And finally, U.
  AUDIO: These are your five vowels! Let's learn more about them.

## [teach] heading: "The Vowel Sounds"
  AUDIO: Each vowel makes a short sound. A is for apple.
  AUDIO: E is for egg. I is for igloo.
  AUDIO: O is for octopus. U is for umbrella.
  AUDIO: Five vowels, five sounds! A, E, I, O, U!

## [teach] heading: "Short Sounds & Long Sounds"
  AUDIO: Vowels can make two sounds: a short sound and a long sound.
  AUDIO: The short A sound. Like in cat.
  AUDIO: The long A sound. Like in cake. The long sound says its name!
  AUDIO: Hear the difference? Cat. Cake. Short A and long A.

## [tip] heading: "Magic E"
  AUDIO: Here is a magic trick called Magic E.
  AUDIO: Look. We have the word cap. Cap has a short A sound.
  AUDIO: Watch this magic! Add an E to the end. Cap becomes cape. The E is silent, but it makes the A say its name.
  AUDIO: That is Magic E. Silent at the end, but it changes the vowel sound!

## [example] heading: "Let's Find the Vowels!"
  AUDIO: Which vowel do you hear in the word 'cat'?
    SCREEN: Which vowel is in 'cat'?
  AUDIO: The vowel in cat is A.
  AUDIO: What about 'dog'? The vowel is O.

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick about vowels.
  AUDIO: Every single word must have at least one vowel! A, E, I, O, U.
    SCREEN: A E I O U
  AUDIO: That is why these five letters are super important. Every word needs one!
