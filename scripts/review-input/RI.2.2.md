# RI.2.2 — Finding the Big Idea  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Find the Big Idea"
  AUDIO: Longer fact texts have lots of little ideas.
    SCREEN: Lots of little ideas
  AUDIO: But one big idea holds them all together.
    SCREEN: One big idea
  AUDIO: That big idea is called the main topic.
    SCREEN: Main topic

## [teach] heading: "Each Paragraph Focuses"
  AUDIO: Each paragraph focuses on a smaller part of the big idea.
    SCREEN: Paragraphs: smaller idea
  AUDIO: Paragraph one might tell what something is.
    SCREEN: Paragraph 1: what it is
  AUDIO: Paragraph two might tell why it matters.
    SCREEN: Paragraph 2: why it matters
  AUDIO: All paragraphs point back to the main topic.
    SCREEN: All paragraphs: main topic

## [example] heading: "Honey Bees"
  AUDIO: Imagine a text about honey bees.
  AUDIO: One paragraph on how they make honey. Another on where they live.
    SCREEN: One paragraph on how they make honey. Another on where they live.
  AUDIO: Main topic? Honey bees! Everything connects back to them.
    SCREEN: Main topic? / Honey bees!

## [tip] heading: "Ask What It Is All About"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: After you read, ask: what is this text mostly about?
    SCREEN: What is it about?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to find the big idea!
