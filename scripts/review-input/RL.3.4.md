    SCREEN: Words have special meanings
  AUDIO: Literal means exactly what it says. Nonliteral is a picture in words.

## [teach] heading: "Raining Cats and Dogs"
  AUDIO: Raining cats and dogs does not really mean animals falling!
    SCREEN: Not animals falling
  AUDIO: It is a nonliteral way to say it is raining really hard.
    SCREEN: Raining really hard

## [example] heading: "Her Heart Raced"
  AUDIO: Her heart raced! Her heart did not run a real race.
    SCREEN: Her heart raced
  AUDIO: It means she was very excited or scared!
    SCREEN: Very excited or scared

## [tip] heading: "Ask: Does It Make Real Sense?"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
