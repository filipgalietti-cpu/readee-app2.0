  AUDIO: The word crash lets you hear the sound.
    SCREEN: Crash / We hear it
  AUDIO: The word sticky lets you feel it on your hands.
    SCREEN: Sticky / We feel it

    SCREEN: Which sense? / We hear it!

## [tip] heading: "Ask Your Senses"
  AUDIO: Here's a trick. Ask, can I hear it, see it, or feel it?
    SCREEN: Hear, see, or feel it?
  AUDIO: The answer tells you the sense!
    SCREEN: That's the sense
