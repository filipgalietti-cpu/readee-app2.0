# RF.K.1a — Reading Left to Right  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Learn to Read Left to Right!"
  AUDIO: Hey there! Did you know that when we read, we always start on the left side and move to the right?
    SCREEN: Read left to right
  AUDIO: It's like following a path! Your eyes start on the left and walk to the right.
    SCREEN: Follow a path
  AUDIO: Let's practice reading the right way!

## [teach] heading: "How Do We Read?"
  AUDIO: When we read, we start on the left side of the page. Then we move across to the right side.
    SCREEN: Start left, move right
  AUDIO: Left to right. That is how we read every line.
    SCREEN: Left to right
  AUDIO: When you get to the end of a line, go down. Then start on the left of the next line.
    SCREEN: Go down, start left

## [example] heading: "Let's Try It Together!"
  AUDIO: Watch my finger! I'll show you how to follow the words.
  AUDIO: I start here on the left. I read the cat sat.
    SCREEN: The cat sat.
  AUDIO: Now I drop down to the next line. I start on the left again. On a mat.
    SCREEN: On a mat.

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Here's a trick to help you read.
  AUDIO: Put your finger under the first word. Then slide it to the right as you read.
    SCREEN: Slide finger to right
  AUDIO: Your finger helps your eyes know where to go.
    SCREEN: Finger helps eyes track
