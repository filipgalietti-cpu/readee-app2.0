  AUDIO: Great readers think before they read.

## [teach] heading: "Two Big Reasons"
  AUDIO: We read for two big reasons.
  AUDIO: A storybook. We read it for fun!
    SCREEN: Storybook: for fun
  AUDIO: A fact book. We read it to learn!
    SCREEN: Fact book: to learn

## [example] heading: "Picking a Book"
  AUDIO: Imagine you want to learn how butterflies fly.
  AUDIO: Which book fits? A fact book about butterflies!
    SCREEN: Which book? / Fact book!
  AUDIO: Now imagine you want a bedtime story.
  AUDIO: A storybook! Your purpose picks your book.
    SCREEN: Purpose picks book

## [tip] heading: "Pause Before You Start"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Before you open a book, ask yourself: what do I want from this book?
    SCREEN: What do I want?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to read with purpose!
