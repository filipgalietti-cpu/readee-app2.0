# RL.K.7 — Pictures Tell the Story  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Pictures Tell the Story!"
  AUDIO: Did you know that pictures in a book can tell you what's happening too?
  AUDIO: Even before you read the words, the pictures give you clues about the story!
    SCREEN: Pictures are Clues!
  AUDIO: Let's learn how to read pictures like a pro!

## [teach] heading: "How Do Pictures Help?"
  AUDIO: Pictures in a storybook show us what the characters look like and what they're doing.
  AUDIO: They show us the setting — where the story is happening!
    SCREEN: Setting
  AUDIO: And they show us how the characters feel. Is the character happy? Sad? Excited?
    SCREEN: Feelings
  AUDIO: So pictures and words work together to tell the whole story!

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Let's try this together! We'll look for clues, say what we see, and tell how we know.
  AUDIO: I see a kid playing outside. What's the setting? It looks like a rainy day!
    SCREEN: Setting
  AUDIO: How does the kid feel? Look at that big smile — happy and having fun!
    SCREEN: Feeling
  AUDIO: See how much we learned just from one picture? You're amazing at this!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Whenever you see a picture in a book, ask yourself two things.
  AUDIO: First, ask: What's happening in the picture?
    SCREEN: Setting
  AUDIO: Then ask: How do the characters feel?
    SCREEN: Feeling
  AUDIO: If you do that, you'll understand the story even better!
