# RL.K.2 — Retelling Stories  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Learn About Retelling!"
  AUDIO: Hey there! Have you ever told someone about a really cool thing that happened to you?
  AUDIO: That is called retelling! When we retell a story, we say what happened in our own words.
  AUDIO: We talk about the beginning, the middle, and the end. Let's learn how!
    SCREEN: Beginning / Middle / End

## [teach] heading: "How Do We Retell a Story?"
  AUDIO: Retelling a story is easy! You just think about what happened in order.
    SCREEN: Retell: what happened in order
  AUDIO: First, what happened at the beginning of the story?
    SCREEN: Beginning?
  AUDIO: Next, what happened in the middle?
    SCREEN: Middle?
  AUDIO: And last, how did the story end? That's it! Beginning, middle, end. You've got this!
    SCREEN: Beginning / Middle / End

## [teach] heading: "Don't Forget the Middle!"
  AUDIO: The middle is the biggest part of the story. It is everything that happens after the beginning, before the very end.
    SCREEN: Middle = the big part
  AUDIO: Say a puppy got lost at the start. In the middle, the puppy looks and looks for home. The middle holds the action! Always tell the middle, not just the beginning and end.
    SCREEN: What's in the middle? / The action!

## [example] heading: ""
  AUDIO: Here's a little story. Listen carefully!
  AUDIO: A bird built a nest. Then she laid three eggs. At last, the baby birds hatched!
    SCREEN: A bird built a nest. Then she laid three eggs. At last, the baby birds hatched!
  AUDIO: Now let's retell it! What happened first? First, a bird built a nest!
    SCREEN: What first? / Bird built nest!
  AUDIO: What happened next? Next, she laid eggs!
    SCREEN: What next? / She laid eggs!
  AUDIO: And last? The babies hatched! You just retold a whole story! Amazing!
    SCREEN: What last? / Babies hatched!

## [tip] heading: "A Secret Trick!"
  AUDIO: Here's a secret trick for retelling stories.
  AUDIO: Use the magic words: first, next, and last. These words help you put everything in the right order!
    SCREEN: First / Next / Last
  AUDIO: Try it every time you hear a story. First this happened, next this, and last that!
