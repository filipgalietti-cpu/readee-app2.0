## [teach] heading: "PH Makes F"
  AUDIO: When P and H team up, they make the F sound!
    SCREEN: PH makes F sound
  AUDIO: Phone. Photo. Dolphin. All PH words that sound like F.
    SCREEN: Phone / Photo / Dolphin

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a tricky word.
  AUDIO: Here is the word: thumb.
    SCREEN: thumb
  AUDIO: Which letter is silent and hides? The B!
    SCREEN: Which letter hides? / The B!
  AUDIO: Do we say it out loud? No, it stays silent!
    SCREEN: Do we say it? / No, it's silent!
  AUDIO: So thumb sounds like thum. The B is just for show. You caught the surprise!
