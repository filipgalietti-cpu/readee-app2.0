# RF.K.2c — Beginning and Ending Sounds  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Beginning and Ending Sounds!"
  AUDIO: Every word starts with a sound and ends with a sound!
  AUDIO: Like 'dog' — it starts with the duh sound and ends with the guh sound!
  AUDIO: Let's learn to hear the beginning and ending sounds of words!

## [teach] heading: "Listening for Sounds"
  AUDIO: The beginning sound is the very first sound you hear in a word.
  AUDIO: Say 'ball.' Ball starts with the letter B.
  AUDIO: The ending sound is the very last sound. Say 'ball.' Ball ends with the letter L.
  AUDIO: Beginning is first, ending is last. Easy!

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Okay, let's try one together! I'll say a word, and we'll find the beginning and ending sounds.
  AUDIO: Here is our word: pig. Say it slowly with me. Pig.
    SCREEN: pig
  AUDIO: What is the very first sound you hear in pig? That's right, Puh! Pig starts with the letter P.
    SCREEN: First sound? / Puh!
  AUDIO: Now what is the very last sound in it? Guh! Pig ends with the letter G.
    SCREEN: Last sound? / Guh!
  AUDIO: So pig starts with Puh and ends with Guh. You found the beginning and ending sounds. Amazing job!

## [example] heading: "Let's Practice Listening!"
  AUDIO: I'll say a word. You tell me the beginning and ending sounds!
  AUDIO: Say 'sun.' Sun starts with the letter S.
  AUDIO: Sun ends with the letter N.
  AUDIO: Sun! You got it!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Stretch the word out like a rubber band!
  AUDIO: Say the word really slowly. Ssss-uuu-nnn. The first sound pops out and the last sound pops out!
  AUDIO: Stretching words helps you hear every sound!
