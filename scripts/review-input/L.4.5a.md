# L.4.5a — Similes and Metaphors  (grade 4th Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Comparing With Pictures"
  AUDIO: Similes and metaphors compare two things to paint a picture.
    SCREEN: Compare to paint picture
  AUDIO: This helps us understand ideas better and makes our writing more interesting!
    SCREEN: Understand, writing interesting

## [teach] heading: "Simile = Like or As"
  AUDIO: A simile uses like or as.
    SCREEN: Simile: like or as
  AUDIO: As busy as a bee.
    SCREEN: As busy as a bee.

## [teach] heading: "Metaphor = Is"
  AUDIO: A metaphor says one thing IS another.
    SCREEN: Metaphor = one thing IS another
  AUDIO: My heart is a star.
    SCREEN: My heart is a star.

## [tip] heading: "Spot the Bridge Word"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: See like or as? Simile. See is or are connecting unlike things? Metaphor.
    SCREEN: Simile: like/as; Metaphor: is/are
  AUDIO: Now you are ready for similes and metaphors!
