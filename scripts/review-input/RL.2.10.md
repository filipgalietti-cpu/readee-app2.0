# RL.2.10 — Reading Stories All Year  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Sticking With Big Stories"
  AUDIO: This year your stories get longer and trickier. Sometimes a part feels confusing.
    SCREEN: Stories get trickier
  AUDIO: Good readers do not just keep going. They use a trick called rereading.
    SCREEN: Use rereading
  AUDIO: To reread means to read a tricky part again, slowly, to understand it.
    SCREEN: Reread = read it again

## [teach] heading: "Stop and Reread"
  AUDIO: When a part feels confusing, stop. Do not rush past it.
    SCREEN: Feel confused? Stop.
  AUDIO: Read that part again, and ask yourself: what is happening here?
    SCREEN: Read again. What is happening?

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here is a tricky little part of a story.
    SCREEN: A tricky part
  AUDIO: Sam froze. The shadow on the wall was huge. Then his dog wagged its tail.
    SCREEN: Sam froze. The shadow was huge. Then his dog wagged its tail.
  AUDIO: Wait, that was confusing. What made the shadow? Let's stop and reread.
    SCREEN: Confused? / Stop and reread.
  AUDIO: Reading again, the dog wagged its tail. So the dog made the big shadow!
    SCREEN: What made the shadow? / The dog!
  AUDIO: Rereading helped us understand. Now the tricky part makes sense!
    SCREEN: Rereading made it make sense!

## [tip] heading: "Back Up and Read Again"
  AUDIO: Here's the trick: if your brain says huh, back up and read it again.
    SCREEN: Say huh? Back up!
