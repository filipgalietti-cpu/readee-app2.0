# RF.2.3d — Prefixes and Suffixes  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Word Add-Ons"
  AUDIO: Little pieces stick on the front and back of words.
    SCREEN: Word add-ons
  AUDIO: The front ones are prefixes. The back ones are suffixes.
    SCREEN: Prefixes: front, Suffixes: back
  AUDIO: Each one changes the word's meaning!
    SCREEN: Changes word meaning

## [teach] heading: "UN and RE Prefixes"
  AUDIO: UN means not. Unhappy means not happy.
  AUDIO: RE means again. Redo means do again.

## [teach] heading: "ER and LY Suffixes"
  AUDIO: ER means one who does. Teacher means one who teaches.
    SCREEN: ER: one who does
  AUDIO: LY turns a word into how. Quickly means in a quick way.
    SCREEN: LY: turns word into how

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a word with an add-on.
  AUDIO: Here is the word: unlock.
    SCREEN: unlock
  AUDIO: What is the prefix at the front? UN!
    SCREEN: What's the prefix? / UN!
  AUDIO: What does it mean? Not!
    SCREEN: What does it mean? / Not!
  AUDIO: So unlock means not locked. You peeled off the prefix!

## [tip] heading: "Peel It Apart"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you meet a long word, peel off the prefix and suffix. Find the root!
    SCREEN: Peel prefix/suffix, find root
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to read prefixes and suffixes!
