## [teach] heading: "UN, RE, PRE"
  AUDIO: Unhappy. The un at the start means not. Unhappy means not happy.
  AUDIO: Redo. The re at the start means again. Redo means do again.
  AUDIO: Preview. The pre at the start means before. Preview means see before.

## [teach] heading: "DIS, MIS, SUB"
  AUDIO: Disagree. The dis at the start means opposite. Disagree means not agree.
  AUDIO: Misspell. The mis at the start means wrongly. Misspell means spell wrong.
  AUDIO: Sub at the start means under. Submarine means under water.

  AUDIO: So dislike means not like. Prefix power!

## [tip] heading: "Peel the Prefix"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
    SCREEN: A helpful trick
  AUDIO: Peel the prefix. What is left is the root. Add the prefix's meaning back!
    SCREEN: unhappy /   →  un + happy /   =  not happy
