# RL.K.4 — Discovering New Words  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Learn About New Words!"
  AUDIO: Sometimes when we read, we find words we don't know yet. And that's totally okay!
  AUDIO: We can use clues in the story to figure out what new words mean. It's like being a word detective!
    SCREEN: Use clues: word detective
  AUDIO: Let's learn how to crack the code on tricky words!

## [teach] heading: "How Do We Figure Out New Words?"
  AUDIO: When you see a word you don't know, don't worry! Look around it for clues.
    SCREEN: Look for clues
  AUDIO: You can look at the other words in the sentence. They can give you hints!
    SCREEN: Other words in sentence
  AUDIO: You can also look at the pictures. Pictures are great clues!
    SCREEN: Look at pictures
  AUDIO: Put the clues together and you can guess what the tricky word means!
    SCREEN: Put clues together

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Here's a story with a tricky word. Let's be word detectives!
  AUDIO: The bunny was famished, so he ate five carrots.
    SCREEN: The bunny was famished, so he ate five carrots.
  AUDIO: Hmm, what does famished mean? Let's look at the clues. He ate FIVE carrots! That's a lot!
    SCREEN: Look for clues
  AUDIO: Famished must mean very hungry! Great detective work!
    SCREEN: Famished = very hungry

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: If you find a tricky word, here's what to do.
  AUDIO: First, read the sentence again.
    SCREEN: Read sentence again
  AUDIO: Then, look at what's happening in the story.
    SCREEN: Look for story clues
  AUDIO: The clues around the word will help you figure it out. You're a word detective now!
    SCREEN: Clues help you
