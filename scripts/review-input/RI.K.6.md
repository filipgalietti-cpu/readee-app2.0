# RI.K.6 — Who Made This Fact Book?  (grade Kindergarten)

  AUDIO: Have you ever wondered who makes the fact books you read? Let's find out!

    SCREEN: Author and illustrator

  AUDIO: The illustrator draws the PICTURES. They show you what the words say!

  AUDIO: Together, they teach you everything in the book!
    SCREEN: Together: the book

## [example] heading: "Let's Find the Author!"
  AUDIO: Look at this book about space. Who wrote it?
  AUDIO: Right there on the cover — by Dr. Mia Orion! That's the author's name.
    SCREEN: Author: Dr. Orion
  AUDIO: Dr. Orion wants us to learn about planets and rockets. That's her message!
    SCREEN: Author's message

## [example] heading: "Let's Find the Illustrator!"
  AUDIO: Look at this book about jungle animals. Who drew the pictures?
  AUDIO: Right there on the cover — pictures by Leo Bright! That's the illustrator's name.
    SCREEN: Illustrator: Leo Bright
  AUDIO: Leo drew lions, tigers, and monkeys so we could see the jungle animals!
    SCREEN: Illustrator draws pictures

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When you read a fact book, ask yourself three questions!
    SCREEN: Ask three questions
  AUDIO: First ask: Who wrote it? That tells you the AUTHOR!
    SCREEN: Who wrote it? → AUTHOR
  AUDIO: Next ask: Who drew the pictures? That tells you the ILLUSTRATOR!
    SCREEN: Who drew it? → ILLUSTRATOR
  AUDIO: Last ask: What are they teaching me? That's the MESSAGE!
    SCREEN: What teaching? → MESSAGE
  AUDIO: Those three questions help you understand any fact book!
    SCREEN: Three questions help
