# RL.K.3 — Characters, Settings, and Events  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Learn About Stories!"
  AUDIO: Every story has three special parts. Do you know what they are?
  AUDIO: Characters, settings, and events! Characters are who the story is about.
    SCREEN: Characters / Settings / Events
  AUDIO: The setting is where it happens. And events are what happens. Let's learn about all three!
    SCREEN: Setting = where

## [teach] heading: "Characters, Settings, and Events"
  AUDIO: Let's break it down! Characters can be people or animals in a story.
  AUDIO: The setting is the place and time. Like a school, a beach, or a forest!
  AUDIO: Events are the things that happen in the story. Like a race, a party, or an adventure!
  AUDIO: So remember: who, where, and what happened. Those are the three parts!

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Okay, let's try one together! Here's a little story.
  AUDIO: A fox napped under a tall tree. A bird sang above her.
    SCREEN: A fox napped under a tall tree. A bird sang above her.
  AUDIO: Who is the story about? That's right, the fox!
    SCREEN: Who? / The fox!
  AUDIO: Where did it happen? Under a tree!
    SCREEN: Where? / Under a tree!
  AUDIO: And what happened? The fox took a nap! You found the character, setting, and event. Amazing job!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When you read a story, ask yourself three questions.
  AUDIO: Who is in it? Where are they? And what do they do?
    SCREEN: Who? / Where? / What?
  AUDIO: Those three questions help you find the characters, setting, and event every single time!
