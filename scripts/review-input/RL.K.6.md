# RL.K.6 — Authors and Illustrators  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Learn About Who Makes Books!"
  AUDIO: Did you know that real people make the books we read? How cool is that!
  AUDIO: One person writes the words. Another person draws the pictures. They work together!
    SCREEN: Writes words, draws pictures
  AUDIO: Let's find out what we call these amazing people!

## [teach] heading: "Authors and Illustrators"
  AUDIO: The person who writes the story is called the author.
  AUDIO: The author thinks up the characters, the setting, and everything that happens!
  AUDIO: The person who draws the pictures is called the illustrator.
  AUDIO: Together, the author and illustrator make a whole book! Pretty amazing, right?

## [example] heading: "Let's See an Example!"
  AUDIO: Look at a book. Can you find who made it?
  AUDIO: On the cover, it says 'Written by' — that's the author! They wrote all the words.
    SCREEN: Author
  AUDIO: And it says 'Illustrated by' — that's the illustrator! They drew all the pictures.
    SCREEN: Illustrator
  AUDIO: Now you know how to find out who made any book. Detective skills!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Next time you pick up a book, look at the cover!
  AUDIO: Find the name after 'by' or 'written by' — that's the author.
    SCREEN: Author
  AUDIO: Now look for 'illustrated by.' That's the illustrator! They drew all the pictures.
    SCREEN: Illustrator
