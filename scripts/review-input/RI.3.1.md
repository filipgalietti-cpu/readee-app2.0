# RI.3.1 — Asking Smart Fact Questions  (grade 3rd Grade)

  AUDIO: Third-grade readers back up answers with the text itself.

## [teach] heading: "Six Big Questions"
  AUDIO: Who, what, where, when, why, how. Ask them all.
    SCREEN: Who / What / Where / When / Why
  AUDIO: Why and how go deepest.
    SCREEN: Why and how go deepest

## [example] heading: "Cite the Text"
  AUDIO: Where do bees live? Say: the text says bees live in hives.
    SCREEN: Where do bees live? / Text says: in hives.
  AUDIO: Always name what the text actually said.
    SCREEN: Always name what text said.

  AUDIO: Use the words: according to the text, to prove your point.
