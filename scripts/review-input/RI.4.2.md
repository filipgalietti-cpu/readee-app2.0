# RI.4.2 — Main Idea Mastery  (grade 4th Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Main Idea + Details"
  AUDIO: Main idea is the umbrella.
    SCREEN: Main idea = umbrella
  AUDIO: Details are the rain underneath.
    SCREEN: Details = rain

## [teach] heading: "Summarize"
  AUDIO: Summarize: main idea plus the most important details.
    SCREEN: Summarize: main idea + details

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Find what the whole passage is mostly about.
  AUDIO: Bees collect nectar. They make honey. They help flowers grow. Bees are busy helpers.
    SCREEN: Bees are busy helpers
  AUDIO: What is the main idea? Bees are helpers. That ties every sentence together.
    SCREEN: What's the main idea? / Bees are helpers.
  AUDIO: Is making honey the main idea? No, it is just one detail under the big idea.
    SCREEN: Is honey the main idea? / No, it's a detail.
  AUDIO: The main idea is the umbrella; honey is rain underneath. You found the heart of the text!

## [example] heading: "Across Multiple Paragraphs"
  AUDIO: In long texts, find the main idea that ties paragraphs together.
    SCREEN: Main idea ties paragraphs

## [tip] heading: "Two-Sentence Test"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Try to summarize the whole text in two sentences. Forces you to find the heart!
    SCREEN: Two-sentence summary
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to master main ideas!
