# RF.1.2d — Breaking Words Into Sounds  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Break It Apart"
  AUDIO: Look! Words are built from smaller sounds.
    SCREEN: Words are built from sounds
  AUDIO: When we take a word apart sound by sound, we call that breaking.
    SCREEN: Breaking a word
  AUDIO: Let us break some words together!
    SCREEN: Let us break some!

## [teach] heading: "Break the Word CAT"
  AUDIO: Here is the word cat.
  AUDIO: Break it into three sounds.
  AUDIO: The first.
  AUDIO: The middle.
  AUDIO: And the last. Three sounds!

## [example] heading: "Count the Sounds"
  AUDIO: How about the word fish?
    SCREEN: Fish
  AUDIO: Let us break it.
  AUDIO: First.
  AUDIO: Middle.
  AUDIO: Last. Fish has three sounds, even though it has four letters!

## [tip] heading: "A Breaking Trick"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Say the word slowly. Tap one finger for each sound you hear.
    SCREEN: Tap for each sound
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to break words into sounds!
    SCREEN: You got it!
