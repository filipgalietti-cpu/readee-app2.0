# RF.1.4c — Using Clues to Read  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Does It Make Sense?"
  AUDIO: Good readers are a little like detectives.
    SCREEN: Readers like detectives
  AUDIO: When a sentence does not make sense, they look for clues.
    SCREEN: Look for clues
  AUDIO: Then they try the word again. Let us see how!
    SCREEN: Try the word again

## [teach] heading: "Stop, Think, Try Again"
  AUDIO: Here are three steps for when a word does not make sense.
  AUDIO: Step one. Stop.
    SCREEN: Step 1: Stop
  AUDIO: Step two. Think. Does that word fit the rest of the sentence?
    SCREEN: Step 2: Think
  AUDIO: Step three. Try again with a word that makes sense.
    SCREEN: Step 3: Try again

## [example] heading: "Spot the Mix-Up"
  AUDIO: Listen. You read, the cat sat on the mat.
    SCREEN: The cat sat on the mat.
  AUDIO: But you said, the cat sat on the map. Uh oh!
    SCREEN: The cat sat on the map. Uh oh!
  AUDIO: Does a cat sit on a map? Stop. Think. That does not make sense.
    SCREEN: Cat on map? / Doesn't make sense!
  AUDIO: Try again. The cat sat on the mat. That makes sense!
    SCREEN: That makes sense!

## [tip] heading: "Always Ask"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: After every sentence, ask yourself, did that make sense?
    SCREEN: Did that make sense?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to practice using clues to read!
