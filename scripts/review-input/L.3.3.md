## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here is a weak word to fix.
  AUDIO: Here is the word: said. The duck said. That word is weak.
    SCREEN: said
  AUDIO: Which word is weak and plain? Said! It does not paint a picture.
    SCREEN: Weak word? / Said.
  AUDIO: What stronger word could we use instead? Quacked! Now you can hear the duck.
    SCREEN: Stronger word? / Quacked!
  AUDIO: So the duck quacked beats the duck said. Strong words paint pictures. Great work!
