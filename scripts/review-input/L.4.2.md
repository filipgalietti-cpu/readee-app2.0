## [teach] heading: "Compound Sentence Commas"
  AUDIO: When two complete sentences join with and, or, but — use a comma before the conjunction.
    SCREEN: Comma before conjunction
  AUDIO: I went to the park, and I had fun.
    SCREEN: I went to the park, and I had fun.
  AUDIO: Here, the comma comes right before the conjunction 'and', connecting the two complete sentences.
    SCREEN: Comma before 'and'

## [teach] heading: "Dialogue and Titles"
  AUDIO: In dialogue, commas and periods go inside the quotation marks.
    SCREEN: Punctuation inside quotes
  AUDIO: In titles, capitalize all important words.
    SCREEN: Capitalize important words
  AUDIO: For example, in the sentence, 'I love reading,' the comma is inside the quotes.
    SCREEN: 'I love reading,'

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here is a sentence that needs a comma.
  AUDIO: Here is the sentence: She ran fast but she fell.
    SCREEN: She ran fast but she fell.
  AUDIO: Which word joins the two complete sentences? But! That is the conjunction.
    SCREEN: The conjunction? / But.
  AUDIO: Where does the comma go? Before it! The comma comes right before the conjunction.
    SCREEN: Comma goes where? / Before it.
  AUDIO: So we write: She ran fast, comma, but she fell. Comma before the conjunction. You nailed it!
