# RL.1.6 — Who's Telling the Story  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Who Is the Storyteller?"
  AUDIO: Every story has a storyteller.
    SCREEN: Every story has storyteller
  AUDIO: Sometimes it is a character in the story. Sometimes it is a voice outside.
    SCREEN: Character or voice outside
  AUDIO: Let us learn how to tell!
    SCREEN: How to tell

## [teach] heading: "The I Clue"
  AUDIO: If the story uses the word I, a character is telling it.
    SCREEN: Story uses 'I'
  AUDIO: Listen. I went to the store with my mom.
    SCREEN: I went to the store with my mom.
  AUDIO: The person saying I is the one telling the story. We call that first person.
    SCREEN: First person: 'I' tells

## [teach] heading: "The He She They Clue"
  AUDIO: If the story uses he, she, or they, a voice outside is telling it.
    SCREEN: Outside voice
  AUDIO: Listen. Sam ran to the park. He played on the swings.
    SCREEN: Sam ran to the park. He played on the swings.
  AUDIO: Someone is watching Sam and telling us. That is the narrator.
    SCREEN: Narrator

## [tip] heading: "Listen for the Clue"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Look at the first sentence. Does it say I, or does it say he, she, or they?
    SCREEN: Look at first sentence
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to spot the storyteller!
