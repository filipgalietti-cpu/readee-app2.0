## [teach] heading: "EA and EE"
  AUDIO: EA says EE, like in eat or read.
  AUDIO: EE says EE too, like in bee or tree.
  AUDIO: Two different spellings, same sound!
    SCREEN: Same sound, two ways!

## [teach] heading: "AI, AY, OA"
  AUDIO: AI says AY, like in rain or train.
  AUDIO: OA says OH, like in boat or road.
  AUDIO: AY says A too. Like in day or play.
