## [teach] heading: "Choose Precise Words"
  AUDIO: Good writers choose just the right words to make their stories shine.
    SCREEN: Choose just right words
  AUDIO: Using precise words helps your reader imagine exactly what is happening.
    SCREEN: Precise words help imagine
  AUDIO: For example, instead of 'walked,' you could say 'tiptoed' if someone was quiet.
    SCREEN: Walked → tiptoed
  AUDIO: Or, instead of 'said,' you might use 'whispered' if they spoke softly.
    SCREEN: Said → whispered
  AUDIO: These precise words paint a clearer picture and make your writing more exciting!
    SCREEN: Paint clearer picture
