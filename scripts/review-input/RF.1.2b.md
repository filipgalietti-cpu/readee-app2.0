# RF.1.2b — Blending Sounds Into Words  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Blending Sounds"
  AUDIO: Listen! Every word is made of little sounds, hiding one right after another.
    SCREEN: Words are made of sounds
  AUDIO: When we push those sounds together into one word, we call it blending.
    SCREEN: Push sounds together = blending
  AUDIO: Use your ears for this one. Let's blend some sounds into real words!
    SCREEN: Use your ears!

## [teach] heading: "Hear Each Sound"
  AUDIO: Listen to three sounds, one at a time. Mmm. Aaa. Nnn.
    SCREEN: mmm... aaa... nnn
  AUDIO: Now push them together fast. Mmm-aaa-nnn. Man! That is blending.
    SCREEN: man

## [example] heading: "Let Us Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's blend one together. Close your eyes and just listen.
  AUDIO: Here are the three sounds. Mmm. Ooo. P.
    SCREEN: mmm... ooo... p
  AUDIO: How many sounds did you hear? Three sounds!
    SCREEN: How many sounds? / Three!
  AUDIO: Now push them together fast. Mmm-ooo-p. Mop!
    SCREEN: Blend them? / mop
  AUDIO: You blended mmm, ooo, p into mop. Blending power!

## [tip] heading: "Slow Then Fast"
  AUDIO: Here's the trick. Say each sound slowly. Sss-iii-t.
    SCREEN: Say it slow
  AUDIO: Then say them faster and faster until they snap together. Sit!
    SCREEN: Then say it fast
