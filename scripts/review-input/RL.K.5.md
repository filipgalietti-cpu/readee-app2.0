## [teach] heading: "Storybooks and Poems"
  AUDIO: A storybook tells a story with characters and events from beginning to end.
  AUDIO: It usually has a beginning, a middle, and an end. Like an adventure!
  AUDIO: A poem uses special words that sometimes rhyme! Poems can be short and fun to read out loud.
  AUDIO: So storybooks are longer adventures, and poems are short and musical!

## [example] heading: "Let's See the Difference!"
  AUDIO: Let me show you an example of each!
  AUDIO: Once upon a time, a frog found a crown. That's from a storybook! It's telling a story.
    SCREEN: Storybook
  AUDIO: Roses are red, violets are blue. That's a poem! Poems use short lines to describe things or share feelings.
    SCREEN: Poem

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Let's learn how to tell storybooks and poems apart by what they are about and how they look.
  AUDIO: Storybooks tell about characters, where they are, and what happens to them.
    SCREEN: Storybooks: characters
  AUDIO: Poems often have short lines, a rhythm, and share feelings or ideas.
    SCREEN: Poems: short lines
