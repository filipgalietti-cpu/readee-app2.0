## [intro] heading: "Little Word Parts"
  AUDIO: Little word parts can change what a word means.
    SCREEN: Word parts change meaning
  AUDIO: They stick on the beginning or the end.
    SCREEN: Beginning or end
  AUDIO: Let us meet a few!

## [teach] heading: "The ER Ending"
  AUDIO: The ending E R can mean one who does.
    SCREEN: ER means one who does
  AUDIO: Teacher? One who teaches!
    SCREEN: Teacher: one who teaches
  AUDIO: Runner? One who runs!
    SCREEN: Runner: one who runs

## [teach] heading: "The UN Beginning"
  AUDIO: The beginning U N means not.
    SCREEN: UN means not
  AUDIO: Unhappy? Not happy!
    SCREEN: Unhappy = not happy
  AUDIO: Unlock? Not locked!
    SCREEN: Unlock = not locked

## [tip] heading: "A Word Part Trick!"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick to understand long words.
  AUDIO: Sometimes, little parts at the end of a word can change its meaning.
    SCREEN: Endings change meaning
  AUDIO: The word 'jump' means to leap. When we add the ending '-ed', it becomes 'jumped', meaning it already happened!
    SCREEN: '-ed' means already happened
  AUDIO: Now you know how word endings can give you clues about what a word means!
