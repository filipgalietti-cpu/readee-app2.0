# RL.3.6 — My View vs the Narrator's  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Two Views"
  AUDIO: The narrator has a view. You have your own view too!
    SCREEN: Narrator's view, your view
  AUDIO: They might agree. They might not!
    SCREEN: Agree or not?

## [teach] heading: "What the Narrator Thinks"
  AUDIO: The narratortells the storyfrom their view.
    SCREEN: Narrator's view
  AUDIO: Their feelingsand opinionsshow.
    SCREEN: Feelings and opinions show

## [teach] heading: "What You Think"
  AUDIO: Readers have their own feelings and opinions too.
    SCREEN: Readers have opinions
  AUDIO: Good readers notice when they disagree with the narrator!
    SCREEN: Disagree with narrator

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Listen to this little story.
  AUDIO: Rain fell all day. What a wonderful, cozy day, the narrator said.
    SCREEN: Rain fell. Cozy day!
  AUDIO: What does the narrator think about the rain? They think it is cozy and wonderful!
    SCREEN: Narrator's view? / Rain is cozy.
  AUDIO: Whose opinion is that? The narrator's, not yours. You might think rain is gloomy!
    SCREEN: Whose view is it? / The narrator's.
  AUDIO: So we told the two views apart. Great detective work!

## [tip] heading: "Separate the Two"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Ask: is that what the narrator thinks, or what I think?
    SCREEN: Narrator or me?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to tell views apart!
