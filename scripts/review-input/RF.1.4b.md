# RF.1.4b — Reading Out Loud  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Read With Expression"
  AUDIO: Reading out loud is like telling a story.
    SCREEN: Reading = telling a story
  AUDIO: Good readers use their voice to make the words come alive.
    SCREEN: Voice makes words alive
  AUDIO: Let us learn a few tricks to read out loud like a star!

## [teach] heading: "Match the Mark"
  AUDIO: The ending mark tells your voice what to do.
    SCREEN: Ending mark tells voice
  AUDIO: A period means stop. Your voice comes down. The dog runs.
    SCREEN: The dog runs.
  AUDIO: A question mark means your voice goes up. Is the dog fast?
    SCREEN: Is the dog fast?
  AUDIO: An exclamation mark means excited! The dog is so fast!
    SCREEN: The dog is so fast!

## [teach] heading: "Not Too Fast, Not Too Slow"
  AUDIO: Good readers read at just the right speed.
    SCREEN: Read at right speed
  AUDIO: Not too fast. The words get jumbled.
    SCREEN: Not too fast
  AUDIO: Not too slow. The words lose their meaning.
    SCREEN: Not too slow
  AUDIO: Nice and smooth, like talking to a friend.
    SCREEN: Nice and smooth

## [tip] heading: "Sound It Out Like a Star"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Pretend you are reading to someone you love. Use your voice!
    SCREEN: Use your voice!
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to practice reading out loud!
