# RL.1.3 — Characters, Settings, and Events  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Three Parts of a Story"
  AUDIO: Every story is built from three big pieces.
  AUDIO: Characters, setting, and events. The who, where, and what.
    SCREEN: Characters / Setting / Events
  AUDIO: Let us look at each one.

## [teach] heading: "Meet the Character"
  AUDIO: Characters are the who of the story.
    SCREEN: Characters = who
  AUDIO: Listen. The kind old woman fed the birds every morning.
    SCREEN: The kind old woman fed the birds every morning.
  AUDIO: We can tell she is kind and caring just from what she does.
    SCREEN: Kind from what she does

## [teach] heading: "Setting and Events"
  AUDIO: The setting is where and when it happens.
    SCREEN: Setting: where and when
  AUDIO: The old woman lived by the sea, in a tiny cottage. That is the setting!
    SCREEN: The old woman lived by the sea, in a tiny cottage.
  AUDIO: Events are what happens in the story.
    SCREEN: Events = what happens
  AUDIO: Feeding the birds every morning. That is an event!
    SCREEN: Feeding the birds every morning.

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together.
  AUDIO: Tom played with his dog, Max. They ran in the sunny park.
    SCREEN: Tom played with his dog, Max. They ran in the sunny park.
  AUDIO: Who is this story about? Tom!
    SCREEN: Who? / Tom!
  AUDIO: Where did they play? In the park.
    SCREEN: Where? / In the park.
  AUDIO: So, Tom and Max played in the park. We found key details!

## [tip] heading: "Picture It"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you read, picture the character, the setting, and the events in your mind.
    SCREEN: Picture story parts
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to practice finding story parts!
