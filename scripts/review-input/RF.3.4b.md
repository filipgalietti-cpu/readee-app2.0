# RF.3.4b — Reading Prose and Poetry  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Read With Expression"
  AUDIO: Good readers do not read like robots. They read with expression!
    SCREEN: Read with expression
  AUDIO: Expression means using your voice to show feelings, just like talking.
    SCREEN: Voice shows feelings

## [teach] heading: "Let The Words Lead"
  AUDIO: The words tell you how to sound. A scared part should sound scared.
    SCREEN: Match your voice to the words
  AUDIO: An exciting part should sound excited and louder!
    SCREEN: Excited part = excited voice

## [teach] heading: "Watch The Marks"
  AUDIO: Punctuation marks give you clues. An exclamation point means say it with energy!
    SCREEN: ! means energy
  AUDIO: A question mark means let your voice go up at the end.
    SCREEN: ? means voice goes up

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here is a line to read.
  AUDIO: Watch out! The wave is coming!
    SCREEN: Watch out! The wave is coming!
  AUDIO: What clue do we see? Exclamation points!
    SCREEN: What clue? / Exclamation points!
  AUDIO: So how should we sound? Excited and urgent!
    SCREEN: How should we sound? / Excited!
  AUDIO: So we read it loud and fast. Watch out! You made it come alive!

## [tip] heading: "Sound It Out Loud"
  AUDIO: Here is a trick. Read it the way the character would say it out loud.
    SCREEN: Read it how they would say it
