# RL.1.5 — Story Books vs Fact Books  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Two Kinds of Books"
  AUDIO: Did you know there are two big kinds of books?
  AUDIO: Story books tell made-up stories.
    SCREEN: Story books = stories
  AUDIO: Fact books give us real information.
    SCREEN: Fact books = facts

## [teach] heading: "Story Books"
  AUDIO: Story books are full of adventures.
    SCREEN: Story books: adventures
  AUDIO: They have characters, a setting, and events that someone imagined.
    SCREEN: Characters
  AUDIO: Dragons, talking animals, magic. Anything goes in a story book!
    SCREEN: Dragons / Talking animals / Magic

## [teach] heading: "Fact Books"
  AUDIO: Fact books teach us about the real world.
    SCREEN: Fact books teach
  AUDIO: Animals, planets, weather, history. All true things!
    SCREEN: All true
  AUDIO: Fact books often have photos, diagrams, and labels.
    SCREEN: Visuals

## [tip] heading: "Which One?"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Could this really happen in real life? Fact book. Made up? Story book!
    SCREEN: Real life? Made up?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to practice telling them apart!
