# RL.4.6 — Different Storytelling Voices  (grade 4th Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Whose Voice?"
  AUDIO: Stories can be told in different voices.
    SCREEN: Different story voices
  AUDIO: First person uses I. Third person uses he, she, they.
    SCREEN: First/Third Person

## [teach] heading: "First Person"
  AUDIO: First person makes you feel close to the character.
    SCREEN: First person: feel close

## [teach] heading: "Third Person"
  AUDIO: Third person feels more like watching from outside.
    SCREEN: Watching from outside
  AUDIO: The narrator is an observer, not a character in the story.
    SCREEN: Narrator: an observer
  AUDIO: You'll often see third-person pronouns like 'he', 'she', 'it', or 'they', or character names being used.
    SCREEN: Pronouns: he / She / They

## [tip] heading: "Notice the Pronouns"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Look at the pronouns in the first sentence. They tell you the voice.
    SCREEN: Pronouns = voice
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to spot storytelling voices!
