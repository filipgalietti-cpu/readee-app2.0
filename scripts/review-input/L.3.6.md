# L.3.6 — Using Conversation Words  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Signal Words"
  AUDIO: Some words are like little signals. They tell your reader when something happens, or where it happens.
    SCREEN: Signal words
  AUDIO: Time words tell when. Place words tell where. Let's learn to use them.
    SCREEN: Time = when. Place = where.

## [teach] heading: "Time Words"
  AUDIO: Time words tell when something happens. Words like before, after, and during.
    SCREEN: before, after, during

## [teach] heading: "Place Words"
  AUDIO: Place words tell where something is. Words like here, there, and behind.
    SCREEN: here, there, behind

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here is a sentence missing a word.
    SCREEN: Fill the missing word
  AUDIO: We packed our bags. Blank, we drove to the lake.
    SCREEN: ____ we drove to the lake.
  AUDIO: Does this signal a time or a place? It tells when, so we need a time word!
    SCREEN: Time or place? / Time!
  AUDIO: After we packed, we drove. After tells when!
    SCREEN: Which word? / After!
  AUDIO: After is a time word. It tells your reader when. Nicely done!
    SCREEN: After tells when.

## [tip] heading: "The Trick"
  AUDIO: Here is the trick. Ask yourself: does this word tell when, or where?
    SCREEN: Ask: when or where?
