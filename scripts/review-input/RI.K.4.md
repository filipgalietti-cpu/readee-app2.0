# RI.K.4 — New Words in Facts  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Learn New Words in Fact Books!"
  AUDIO: Fact books teach us about the world, and sometimes they use words we haven't heard before!
    SCREEN: New words in fact books
  AUDIO: But don't worry — the book gives us clues to figure out what those new words mean!
    SCREEN: Book gives us clues
  AUDIO: Let's learn how to be word explorers!

## [teach] heading: "How to Figure Out New Words"
  AUDIO: When a fact book uses a word you don't know, look around it!
  AUDIO: Read the sentence around the word. It can tell you what the word means!
    SCREEN: Sentence
  AUDIO: Look at the pictures. They show you what the word is about!
    SCREEN: Pictures
  AUDIO: Sentences plus pictures equal word power!

## [example] heading: "Let's Figure One Out!"
  AUDIO: Let's try one! Read along and listen for a new word.
  AUDIO: The Arctic is very frigid. Animals need thick fur to stay warm there.
    SCREEN: The Arctic is very frigid. Animals need thick fur to stay warm there.
  AUDIO: Look at the clues! Thick fur means animals stay warm. And the Arctic has ice and snow.
    SCREEN: Thick fur / Ice & snow
  AUDIO: So frigid must mean really, really cold!
    SCREEN: Frigid means Very Cold!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When you see a new word in a fact book, be a detective!
  AUDIO: Look at the words around it. Look at the pictures. Put the clues together!
    SCREEN: Look for clues
  AUDIO: You're a word explorer now!
