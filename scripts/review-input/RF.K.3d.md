# RF.K.3d — Look-Alike Words  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Look-Alike Words!"
  AUDIO: Some words look almost the same, but they are different!
    SCREEN: Words look almost same
  AUDIO: Look at the word was.
  AUDIO: Now flip the letters around. Same letters, but now it says saw!
    SCREEN: Flip letters: was, saw
  AUDIO: Let us learn how to spot the difference between look-alike words.

## [teach] heading: "How to Tell Them Apart"
  AUDIO: Two tricks help us tell look-alike words apart.
  AUDIO: Trick one. Look at the very first letter. That tells you a lot!
    SCREEN: Look at first letter
  AUDIO: Trick two. Then read the rest of the letters, one at a time.
    SCREEN: Read rest of letters

## [example] heading: "Spot the Difference!"
  AUDIO: Look at these two words. Can you tell them apart?
  AUDIO: 'On' and 'no.' They have the same letters in a different order.
    SCREEN: On and No
  AUDIO: 'On' starts with O. 'No' starts with N. The first letter makes them different words!
    SCREEN: First letter differs

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When words look alike, point to each letter from left to right.
    SCREEN: Point left to right
  AUDIO: Say each letter as you point. That slows you down and helps you read the right word!
    SCREEN: Say each letter
  AUDIO: Slow and careful beats fast and wrong!
    SCREEN: Slow beats fast
