# RI.4.6 — Firsthand vs Secondhand  (grade 4th Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Two Kinds of Accounts"
  AUDIO: A firsthand account is from someone who was there.
    SCREEN: Firsthand: was there
  AUDIO: A secondhand account is from someone who learned about it.
    SCREEN: Secondhand: learned about it

## [teach] heading: "Diary vs Article"
  AUDIO: A diary entry is firsthand.
    SCREEN: Diary entry = firsthand
  AUDIO: A history article is usually secondhand.
    SCREEN: History article = secondhand

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Listen to this passage.
  AUDIO: In 1849, thousands of miners traveled west to find gold.
    SCREEN: In 1849, thousands of miners traveled west to find gold.
  AUDIO: Was the writer there? No!
    SCREEN: Was writer there? / No!
  AUDIO: So what kind of account is it? Secondhand!
    SCREEN: What kind of account? / Secondhand!
  AUDIO: It gives facts and dates, not feelings, so it is secondhand. You figured it out!

## [example] heading: "Different Details"
  AUDIO: Firsthand has personal feelings and senses. Secondhand has more facts and dates.
    SCREEN: Feelings vs. Facts

## [tip] heading: "Ask: Were They There?"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Ask: was the writer actually there? If yes, firsthand!
    SCREEN: Was writer there? / Yes? Firsthand!
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to tell firsthand from secondhand!
