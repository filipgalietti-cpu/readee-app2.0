# RL.4.2 — Discovering Themes  (grade 4th Grade)

## [intro] heading: "What Is the Big Lesson?"
  AUDIO: A theme is the big idea or life lesson the story shares.
    SCREEN: Theme: big idea, lesson
  AUDIO: Themes are not stated. You discover them.
    SCREEN: Themes: discover them

## [teach] heading: "Look at Choices and Endings"
  AUDIO: What did the character learn?
    SCREEN: Character learned?
  AUDIO: What changed at the end?
    SCREEN: End changed?

    SCREEN: Theme in one sentence
