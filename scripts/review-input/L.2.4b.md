# L.2.4b — Prefix Power  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Little Prefix, Big Change"
  AUDIO: A prefix is a small word piece at the front.
    SCREEN: Prefix: small word piece
  AUDIO: Adding one changes the word's meaning.
    SCREEN: Changes word meaning

## [teach] heading: "UN Means Not"
  AUDIO: UN means not. Unhappy is not happy.
    SCREEN: UN means not
  AUDIO: Unlock, unsafe, unkind — all start with UN.
    SCREEN: Words with UN

## [teach] heading: "RE Means Again"
  AUDIO: RE means again. Redo means do again.
    SCREEN: RE means again
  AUDIO: Rewrite, reread, replay.
    SCREEN: Rewrite / Reread / Replay

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a new word.
  AUDIO: Here is the word: preheat.
    SCREEN: preheat
  AUDIO: What is the prefix? PRE!
    SCREEN: What's the prefix? / PRE!
  AUDIO: What does it mean? Before!
    SCREEN: What does it mean? / Before!
  AUDIO: So preheat means heat before. Prefix power!

## [tip] heading: "Peel Off the Prefix"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you see a long word, peel off the prefix. See if the root word looks familiar!
    SCREEN: Peel off prefix
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to use prefix power!
