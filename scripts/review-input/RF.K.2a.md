  AUDIO: Today is going to be really fun! We're learning about rhyming words!

  AUDIO: Rhyming is so much fun, and it helps us play with sounds. Let's learn more!
    SCREEN: Same ending sound means Rhyme!

## [teach] heading: "What Are Rhyming Words?"
  AUDIO: Words rhyme when they have the same ending sound. Let me show you!
    SCREEN: Same ending sound
  AUDIO: Dog and log. Hear that? Dog, log. They both end with the 'og' sound!
    SCREEN: Dog / Log
  AUDIO: Sun and fun. Sun, fun. They both end with the 'un' sound!
    SCREEN: Sun / Fun
  AUDIO: The trick is to listen to the end of each word. If the endings match, they rhyme!
    SCREEN: Endings match? They rhyme!

## [example] heading: "Let's Try Some Together!"
  AUDIO: Okay, let's practice together! I'll say two words, and we'll figure out if they rhyme.
  AUDIO: Cake and lake. Cake, lake. Do they rhyme? Yes! They both end with the 'ake' sound. Nice job!
    SCREEN: Cake / Lake
  AUDIO: Now try this one. Cake and dog. Cake, dog. Do they rhyme? Nope! Those endings sound totally different.
    SCREEN: Cake / Dog
  AUDIO: See? You just have to listen to the ends of the words. You're getting so good at this!
    SCREEN: Listen to the endings!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Here's a super fun trick to remember.
  AUDIO: Say two words out loud and listen really carefully to the ends. If they sound the same at the end, they rhyme!
    SCREEN: Listen to the endings!
  AUDIO: Try it with your own name! What rhymes with your name? Think about it!
    SCREEN: What rhymes with YOUR name?
