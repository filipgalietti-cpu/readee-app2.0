# K.L.4 — Figuring Out Word Meanings  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Figure Out Word Meanings!"
  AUDIO: Sometimes we hear or read a word we don't know. That's totally normal!
  AUDIO: The great news is there are tricks to figure out what new words mean!
    SCREEN: Tricks for new words
  AUDIO: Let's learn how to crack the code!

## [teach] heading: "Clues Are All Around!"
  AUDIO: When you hear a word you don't know, listen to the other words around it.
    SCREEN: Words around it
  AUDIO: The words before and after give you clues about what the new word means.
    SCREEN: Words before and after
  AUDIO: You can also ask a grown-up, or look at pictures for help!
    SCREEN: Ask grown-up, pictures
  AUDIO: Context clues plus pictures plus asking — that's the triple combo!
    SCREEN: Triple combo

## [example] heading: "Let's Try It!"
  AUDIO: Here's a sentence: The rabbit was swift, it ran very fast!
    SCREEN: The rabbit was swift, it ran very fast!
  AUDIO: What does 'swift' mean? Look at the clue — it ran very fast!
    SCREEN: What is swift? / Ran very fast!
  AUDIO: Swift must mean fast! The clue told us right there in the sentence!
    SCREEN: Swift means? / Fast!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Think of yourself as a word detective. Every unknown word is a mystery to solve!
    SCREEN: Word detective
  AUDIO: Read the sentence again, look at pictures, and try to guess. You'll be right more than you think!
    SCREEN: Read / Look / Guess
  AUDIO: Every word you figure out makes you a better reader!
