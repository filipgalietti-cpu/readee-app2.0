## [example] heading: "Retell It"
  AUDIO: Retelling is telling a tale in your own words.
    SCREEN: Retelling: own words
  AUDIO: Start with the characters. Then what happened. Then the lesson!

  AUDIO: Keep the big parts. Skip tiny details.
    SCREEN: Keep big parts, skip details

## [tip] heading: "Find the Lesson"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Ask, what did the character learn? That is your lesson!
