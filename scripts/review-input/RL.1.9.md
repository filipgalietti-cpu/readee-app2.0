  AUDIO: Two stories can be alike in some ways and different in others.
    SCREEN: Stories: same & different
  AUDIO: We compare to see what is the same.
    SCREEN: Compare = same
  AUDIO: We contrast to see what is different.
    SCREEN: Contrast = different

## [teach] heading: "Two Honey Stories"
  AUDIO: Story one. A bear looks for honey.
    SCREEN: A bear looks for honey.
  AUDIO: Story two. A bee makes honey.
    SCREEN: A bee makes honey.
  AUDIO: What is the same? Both are about honey!
    SCREEN: Same: about honey
  AUDIO: What is different? One finds it, one makes it.
    SCREEN: Different: finds, makes

## [teach] heading: "Two Brave Friends"
  AUDIO: Story one. Amy is brave and climbs a mountain.
    SCREEN: Amy is brave and climbs a mountain.
  AUDIO: Story two. Bob is brave and swims a lake.
    SCREEN: Bob is brave and swims a lake.
  AUDIO: Same? Both are brave adventures!
    SCREEN: Both brave adventures
  AUDIO: Different? One climbs, one swims.
    SCREEN: One climbs, one swims

## [tip] heading: "A Comparing Trick"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Ask, what do both stories share? Then ask, what is special about each one?
    SCREEN: What both share? What's special?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to practice comparing stories!
