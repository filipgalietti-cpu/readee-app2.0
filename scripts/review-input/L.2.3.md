## [teach] heading: "Formal and Casual"
  AUDIO: Formal words are for teachers, letters, and essays.
    SCREEN: Formal: teachers, letters
  AUDIO: Casual words are for friends and family.
    SCREEN: Casual: friends, family
  AUDIO: Thank you vs. Thanks. Both are nice. One is more formal.
    SCREEN: Thank you vs. Thanks

## [teach] heading: "Stronger Words"
  AUDIO: Stronger words paint a stronger picture.
    SCREEN: Stronger words paint picture
  AUDIO: Instead of big, try enormous. Instead of sad, try heartbroken.
    SCREEN: Big → enormous, sad → heartbroken

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a sentence.
  AUDIO: Sam won the race. Sam was very happy.
    SCREEN: Sam was very happy.
  AUDIO: Could a stronger word paint a clearer picture than happy? Yes!
    SCREEN: Want a stronger word? / Yes!
  AUDIO: What word is stronger than happy? Thrilled!
    SCREEN: Stronger than happy? / Thrilled!
  AUDIO: So Sam was thrilled paints a stronger picture. You picked the perfect word!

## [tip] heading: "Know Your Reader"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Think about who will read your words. Then pick words that fit!
    SCREEN: Pick words that fit
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to choose words to sound just right!
