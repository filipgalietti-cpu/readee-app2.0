# RF.1.3g — Tricky Sight Words  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Words That Break the Rules"
  AUDIO: Most words you can sound out. But a few are sneaky!
    SCREEN: A few are sneaky!
  AUDIO: We call them sight words. They do not follow the usual sound rules.
    SCREEN: Sight words
  AUDIO: So we cannot sound them out. We learn them by sight and remember them.
    SCREEN: Learn by sight

## [teach] heading: "Said"
  AUDIO: Look at this word. It is spelled S A I D.
    SCREEN: Spelled S-A-I-D
  AUDIO: If we sounded it out, we would say sayed. But it really sounds like sed!
    SCREEN: Sounds like sed
  AUDIO: There is no rule for this one. We just remember it by sight.
    SCREEN: Just remember it

## [teach] heading: "Was and Of"
  AUDIO: Here is was. It is spelled W A S, but it sounds like wuz.
    SCREEN: was = wuz
  AUDIO: Here is of. It is spelled O F, but it sounds like uv.
    SCREEN: of = uv
  AUDIO: We do not sound these out. We just know them by sight.
    SCREEN: Know by sight

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here is a tricky word.
  AUDIO: Here is the word: the.
    SCREEN: the
  AUDIO: How is it spelled? T H E!
    SCREEN: How is it spelled? / T-H-E!
  AUDIO: Can we sound it out? No! We just know it by sight.
    SCREEN: Sound it out? / No, by sight!
  AUDIO: So the is a sight word. We remember it. Great job!

## [tip] heading: "See Them Every Day"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick. The more you see a sight word, the faster you know it.
    SCREEN: See it, know it
  AUDIO: Flash cards help! Soon you will read these words in a snap.
