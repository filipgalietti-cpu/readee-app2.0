# RI.1.2 — Main Topic and Key Details  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "The Big Idea"
  AUDIO: Every fact book is mostly about one big idea.
    SCREEN: One big idea
  AUDIO: That one big idea is called the main topic.
    SCREEN: Main topic
  AUDIO: The little facts that tell us more are called key details.
    SCREEN: Key details

## [teach] heading: "Topic vs Detail"
  AUDIO: Listen. Apples can be red or green. They grow on trees. Apples are a healthy snack.
    SCREEN: Apples can be red or green. They grow on trees.
  AUDIO: What one word do we hear again and again? Apples. So the main topic is apples.
    SCREEN: Main topic? / Apples
  AUDIO: The key details tell us more about apples: their color, where they grow, and that they are healthy.
    SCREEN: Key details? / Color, trees, healthy

## [example] heading: "Let Us Try One"
  AUDIO: Let us try one together. Listen to this little book.
  AUDIO: Firefighters wear special gear. They drive fire trucks. They help put out fires.
    SCREEN: Firefighters wear gear. They drive trucks. They put out fires.
  AUDIO: What is this book mostly about? Firefighters. That is the main topic.
    SCREEN: Main topic? / Firefighters
  AUDIO: Now the key details. What do firefighters wear? Special gear.
    SCREEN: Detail one? / Special gear
  AUDIO: What do they drive? Fire trucks. And what do they do? Put out fires. Three key details, all about firefighters!
    SCREEN: More details? / Trucks, put out fires

## [tip] heading: "What Comes Up Most?"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick. The word that comes up the most is usually the main topic.
    SCREEN: Most-repeated word = topic
  AUDIO: The key details are the little facts that tell you more about that topic.
    SCREEN: Details tell more
