# L.4.1 — Pronouns and Grammar Rules  (grade 4th Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Pronouns That Agree"
  AUDIO: A pronoun is a short word that stands in for a noun, like she, he, it, or they.
    SCREEN: Pronoun = stands for a noun
  AUDIO: The noun the pronoun stands for is called the antecedent.
    SCREEN: Noun it stands for = antecedent
  AUDIO: Today we make the pronoun agree with its antecedent.
    SCREEN: Make them agree

## [teach] heading: "One or Many?"
  AUDIO: One person or thing is singular. Use it, she, or he.
    SCREEN: One: it, she, he
  AUDIO: More than one is plural. Use they or them.
    SCREEN: Many: they, them

## [teach] heading: "Match the Number"
  AUDIO: The pronoun must match its antecedent in number.
    SCREEN: Pronoun matches the noun
  AUDIO: The dogs ate their food. Dogs is plural, so we use their.
    SCREEN: The dogs ate their food.

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a sentence with a missing pronoun.
    SCREEN: Find the pronoun
  AUDIO: The teacher lost blank keys.
    SCREEN: The teacher lost ___ keys.
  AUDIO: What is the antecedent? Teacher! And teacher is just one person.
    SCREEN: Antecedent? / Teacher (one)
  AUDIO: One person is singular, so we need a singular pronoun.
    SCREEN: One or many? / Singular!
  AUDIO: The teacher lost her keys. Her matches teacher. You made them agree!
    SCREEN: Which pronoun? / her

## [tip] heading: "Point Back First"
  AUDIO: Before you pick a pronoun, point back to the noun it replaces.
    SCREEN: Point back to the noun
  AUDIO: Ask one or many? One uses she, he, or it. Many uses they.
    SCREEN: One or many?
