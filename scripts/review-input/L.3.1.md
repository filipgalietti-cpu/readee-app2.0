## [teach] heading: "Tricky Past Tense Verbs"
  AUDIO: Most verbs become past tense by adding 'ed' to the end, like 'walk' becomes 'walked'.
    SCREEN: Verbs add -ed
  AUDIO: But some verbs are tricky and change in a different way.
    SCREEN: Some verbs change
  AUDIO: For example, the past tense of 'go' is not 'goed', it's 'went'.
    SCREEN: Go: went
  AUDIO: And the past tense of 'eat' is not 'eated', it's 'ate'.
    SCREEN: Eat: ate

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a verb.
  AUDIO: Here is the verb: run.
    SCREEN: run
  AUDIO: Do we just add E-D to make runned? No! Run is a tricky verb that changes.
    SCREEN: Is it add -ed? / No, it's tricky!
  AUDIO: What is the past tense of it? Ran!
    SCREEN: Past tense of run? / Ran!
  AUDIO: So run becomes ran, not runned. You caught that tricky verb!
