# RF.3.3c — Multisyllable Words  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Big Words, No Sweat"
  AUDIO: Multisyllable words have three or more syllables.
    SCREEN: Multisyllable: 3+ syllables
  AUDIO: Long words are just small words put together.
    SCREEN: Long words = small words

## [teach] heading: "Clap Them Out"
  AUDIO: Clap each syllable. Re-mem-ber. Three claps. Three syllables!
    SCREEN: Re-mem-ber = 3 claps

## [teach] heading: "Split Strategically"
  AUDIO: Look for vowels. Each syllable has one vowel sound.
    SCREEN: Vowel sound per syllable
  AUDIO: Split between consonants or before a single consonant.
    SCREEN: Split between consonants

## [tip] heading: "Chunk + Read"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Draw slashes between syllables. Read each chunk, then blend.
    SCREEN: Chunk, then blend
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to read long words!
