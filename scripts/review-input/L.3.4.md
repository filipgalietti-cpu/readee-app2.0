## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together, detective. Here is a little story.
  AUDIO: The room was gloomy. No light came in, and Sam felt sad.
    SCREEN: Room gloomy, Sam sad
  AUDIO: What is our mystery word? Gloomy! Let's find clues to crack it.
    SCREEN: Our mystery word? / Gloomy!
  AUDIO: So what does it mean? Dark and sad! The clues said no light came in and Sam felt sad.
    SCREEN: What does it mean? / Dark and sad.
  AUDIO: Gloomy means dark and sad. You cracked the code, detective!

## [tip] heading: "Combine Both"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Combine sentence clues with word parts. You will almost always crack it!
    SCREEN: Combine clues + parts
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to detect word meanings!
