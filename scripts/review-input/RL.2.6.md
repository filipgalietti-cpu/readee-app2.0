# RL.2.6 — Different Points of View  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Every Character Sees Differently"
  AUDIO: In a story, each character has their own way of seeing things.
    SCREEN: Characters see differently
  AUDIO: That is called their point of view.
    SCREEN: Point of view
  AUDIO: Two characters can see the same thing very differently!
    SCREEN: See things differently

## [teach] heading: "Two Views of a Park"
  AUDIO: Tom loves the park. He thinks it is the best place ever!
    SCREEN: Tom loves the park. He thinks it is the best place ever!
  AUDIO: Lea feels lonely at the park. She wishes her friend was there.
    SCREEN: Lea feels lonely at the park. She wishes her friend was there.
  AUDIO: Same park, two very different points of view!
    SCREEN: Two different views

## [example] heading: "Listen for Their Feelings"
  AUDIO: A character's point of view comes from what they feel and want.
    SCREEN: Point of view: feel, want
  AUDIO: Listen to what they say. Watch what they do.
    SCREEN: Say, do = clues
  AUDIO: Those are clues to their point of view!
    SCREEN: Clues to point of view

## [tip] heading: "Walk in Their Shoes"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Imagine you are the character. How would you feel?
    SCREEN: Imagine you are character
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to see different points of view!
