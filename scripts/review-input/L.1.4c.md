# L.1.4c — Root Word Magic  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "The Root Word"
  AUDIO: A root word is the main part of a word.
    SCREEN: Root word: main part
  AUDIO: Other parts can stick on to it.
    SCREEN: Parts stick on
  AUDIO: But the root stays the same.
    SCREEN: Root stays same

## [teach] heading: "Jumping and Jumped"
  AUDIO: Look at these words.
  AUDIO: What is the same in all three?
  AUDIO: Jump! That is the root word.
    SCREEN: Root word: Jump
  AUDIO: I N G, E D, and S are the extra parts.
    SCREEN: ING / ED / S

## [example] heading: "Play Play Play"
  AUDIO: Playing. Played. Plays.
    SCREEN: Playing / Played / Plays
  AUDIO: What is the root word? Play!
    SCREEN: Root word? / Play!
  AUDIO: The endings change, but play stays!
    SCREEN: Endings change, play stays

## [tip] heading: "Find the Root"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Cover up the ending. What is left? That is the root!
    SCREEN: Cover up the ending
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to find root words!
