# RI.K.5 — Parts of a Book  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Learn About Parts of a Book!"
  AUDIO: Did you know a book has special parts, just like your body has parts?
  AUDIO: There's a front cover, a back cover, and the title page! Each part tells you something important.
    SCREEN: Front / Back / Title page
  AUDIO: Let's learn what each part does!

## [teach] heading: "The Parts of a Book"
  AUDIO: The front cover is the very first thing you see. It has the title and usually a picture!
    SCREEN: Front cover: first thing
  AUDIO: The title tells you what the book is about. It's like the book's name!
    SCREEN: Title: book's name
  AUDIO: The back cover is on the other side. It might tell you a little bit about the book.
    SCREEN: Back cover: other side
  AUDIO: The title page is inside the book. It shows the title, author, and illustrator!
    SCREEN: Title page: inside

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together! Here's a new book.
  AUDIO: This book is called All About Bugs.
    SCREEN: All About Bugs
  AUDIO: What part shows the title and a picture? The front cover!
    SCREEN: What part? / Front cover!
  AUDIO: Where do we find who made it? The title page inside!
    SCREEN: Find who made it? / Title page!
  AUDIO: The front cover greets you, and the title page tells who made it. You're a book expert!

## [example] heading: "Let's Find the Parts!"
  AUDIO: Look at this book. Can you find the front cover?
  AUDIO: There it is! The front cover has a whale and says 'Ocean Animals.' That's the title!
    SCREEN: Title
  AUDIO: If we flip it over, we see the back cover!
    SCREEN: Back cover
  AUDIO: And if we open it up, we see the title page inside!
    SCREEN: Title page

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Every time you pick up a new book, check out the parts first!
  AUDIO: Look at the front cover to learn what the book is about. Check the title page for who made it!
    SCREEN: Front Cover, Title Page
  AUDIO: You'll be a book expert in no time!
