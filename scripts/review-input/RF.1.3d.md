# RF.1.3d — Counting Syllables  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Clap Out the Beats"
  AUDIO: Every word has beats inside it. We call them syllables.
    SCREEN: Beats = syllables
  AUDIO: You can clap a word to find the beats!
    SCREEN: Clap for beats
  AUDIO: Let us try some together.

## [teach] heading: "Cat Is One Beat"
  AUDIO: Listen. Cat.
    SCREEN: Cat
  AUDIO: One clap! Cat has one beat. One syllable.
    SCREEN: One Syllable

## [teach] heading: "Rabbit Is Two Beats"
  AUDIO: Listen. Rab-bit.
    SCREEN: Rab-bit.
  AUDIO: Two claps! Rabbit has two beats. Two syllables.
    SCREEN: Two syllables
  AUDIO: Every syllable needs a vowel. Rabbit has A and I. Two vowels, two syllables.
    SCREEN: Syllable needs vowel

## [tip] heading: "Your Hand Knows"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Put your hand under your chin. Each time your chin drops, that is one syllable!
    SCREEN: Chin drops = syllable
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to count syllables!
