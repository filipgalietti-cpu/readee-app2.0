# RF.2.4 — Reading with Flow  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Smooth Reading"
  AUDIO: Great readers read smoothly, like talking.
    SCREEN: Read smoothly
  AUDIO: Fluency is reading accurately, at the right speed, with expression.
    SCREEN: Fluency: accurate / Speed / Expression

## [teach] heading: "Accuracy First"
  AUDIO: Accuracy means reading the words correctly.
    SCREEN: Accuracy: read correctly
  AUDIO: If you get stuck, slow down and sound it out.
    SCREEN: Stuck? Sound it out

## [teach] heading: "Rate and Expression"
  AUDIO: Rate is how fast. Not too fast, not too slow.
    SCREEN: Rate: how fast
  AUDIO: Expression means using your voice to match the story.
    SCREEN: Expression: match voice

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a sentence to read with flow.
  AUDIO: The puppy is lost! Where can he be?
    SCREEN: The puppy is lost! Where can he be?
  AUDIO: How should we read it? With expression, so it sounds worried and curious!
    SCREEN: Read it how? / With expression!
  AUDIO: And the speed? Not too fast, not too slow, just right, like you're talking.
    SCREEN: Too fast or too slow? / Just right!
  AUDIO: Hear how it sounds like real talking? That's reading with flow. You did it!

## [tip] heading: "Read Like You Talk"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Pretend you are telling the story to a friend. Use your normal voice!
    SCREEN: Use normal voice
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to read with flow!
