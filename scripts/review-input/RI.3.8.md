# RI.3.8 — Logical Connections  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "How Sentences Connect"
  AUDIO: Sentences in a text are not random. They link logically.
    SCREEN: Sentences link logically
  AUDIO: Each sentence adds to or follows the one before.
    SCREEN: Sentences add or follow

## [teach] heading: "Signal Words"
  AUDIO: Words like also, however, and because signal connections.
    SCREEN: Signal connections
  AUDIO: They tell you how the next sentence relates.
    SCREEN: How sentences relate

## [example] heading: "However"
  AUDIO: However means a twist or surprise is coming.
    SCREEN: However = twist/surprise
  AUDIO: The day was sunny. However, a storm was coming.
    SCREEN: The day was sunny. However, a storm was coming.

## [tip] heading: "Circle the Signals"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: As you read, circle signal words. They show how ideas connect.
    SCREEN: Circle signal words
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to spot logical connections!
