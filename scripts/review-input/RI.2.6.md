# RI.2.6 — Why Did the Author Write This?  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Authors Have a Purpose"
  AUDIO: Every author writes for a reason.
    SCREEN: Author writes for reason
  AUDIO: We call that the author's purpose.
    SCREEN: Author's purpose
  AUDIO: Usually it is to teach, to entertain, or to convince.
    SCREEN: Teach / Entertain / Convince

## [teach] heading: "To Teach"
  AUDIO: Fact books want to teach us.
    SCREEN: Fact books teach us
  AUDIO: They share facts, steps, and pictures that help us learn.
    SCREEN: Share facts / Steps / Pictures

## [teach] heading: "To Entertain or Convince"
  AUDIO: Stories entertain us. They make us laugh, cry, or wonder.
    SCREEN: Stories entertain us
  AUDIO: Some texts try to convince. They give reasons to agree with the author.
    SCREEN: Texts try to convince

## [tip] heading: "Ask the Big Why"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: When you finish, ask: why did the author write this?
    SCREEN: Why did author write?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to spot author purpose!
