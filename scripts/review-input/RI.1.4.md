# RI.1.4 — Figuring Out New Words  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "New Word? No Problem"
  AUDIO: Fact books sometimes have new words you do not know.
    SCREEN: New words in books
  AUDIO: That is totally fine. Good readers are curious.
    SCREEN: Good readers are curious
  AUDIO: Let us learn how to figure them out!

## [teach] heading: "Look for Clues"
  AUDIO: When you meet a new word, look at the words around it.
    SCREEN: Look around
  AUDIO: The other words give you clues about what it means.
    SCREEN: Other words = clues
  AUDIO: Sometimes the book will even tell you!
    SCREEN: Book tells you

## [example] heading: "What Is a Habitat?"
  AUDIO: Listen. The habitat is the place where an animal lives.
    SCREEN: Habitat = where animal lives
  AUDIO: A fish habitat is water.
    SCREEN: A fish habitat is water.
  AUDIO: What is a habitat? The place where an animal lives! The book told us right in the sentence.
    SCREEN: What is habitat? / Place animal lives!

## [tip] heading: "Ask Questions"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: If you still do not know the word, ask someone or check a dictionary!
    SCREEN: Ask or check dictionary
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to figure out new words!
