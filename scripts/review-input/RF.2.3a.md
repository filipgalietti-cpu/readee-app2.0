# RF.2.3a — Long and Short Vowels  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Two Vowel Voices"
  AUDIO: Every vowel has two voices: short and long.
    SCREEN: Short + long
  AUDIO: Short sounds are quick. Long sounds say the letter's name.
    SCREEN: Quick vs says name
  AUDIO: Let us listen.
    SCREEN: Listen!

## [teach] heading: "Short in CAT"
  AUDIO: The word cat has a short A.
  AUDIO: Short A says ah. Quick!
    SCREEN: Short A: ah

## [teach] heading: "Long in CAKE"
  AUDIO: The word cake has a long A.
  AUDIO: Long A says AY. It says its name!
    SCREEN: Long A: AY
  AUDIO: A silent E at the end often signals a long vowel.
    SCREEN: Silent E = long vowel

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a word.
  AUDIO: Here is the word: kite.
    SCREEN: kite
  AUDIO: Is the vowel long or short? Long I!
    SCREEN: Long or short? / Long I!
  AUDIO: How do you know it's long? It says its own name, eye!
    SCREEN: How do you know? / It says its name.
  AUDIO: Kite has a long I, helped by silent E. You told long from short!

## [tip] heading: "Listen Closely"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
    SCREEN: A vowel trick
  AUDIO: Ask: does the vowel say its name? Long. Or does it make a quick sound? Short.
    SCREEN: Name or quick?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to tell long from short vowels!
    SCREEN: You got it!
