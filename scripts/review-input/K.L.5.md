## [teach] heading: "Opposites and Categories"
  AUDIO: Opposites are words that mean the complete reverse. Big and small. Hot and cold. Up and down!
    SCREEN: Opposites: big/small
  AUDIO: They're like two sides of a coin!
    SCREEN: Two sides of a coin
  AUDIO: Words can also belong to groups. 'Dog,' 'cat,' and 'fish' are all animals!
    SCREEN: Groups: all animals
  AUDIO: And some words mean almost the same thing! 'Big' and 'large' are alike!
    SCREEN: Big = large

## [example] heading: "Let's Find the Connections!"
  AUDIO: What's the opposite of 'happy'?
  AUDIO: Sad! Happy and sad are opposites!
    SCREEN: Sad is opposite
  AUDIO: Now, which group does 'apple' belong in? It's a fruit! Just like banana and orange!
    SCREEN: Apple group? / Fruit

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When you learn a new word, think: What's the opposite? What group does it belong to?
    SCREEN: Opposite? Group?
  AUDIO: Connecting words to other words helps you remember them better!
    SCREEN: Connect words to remember
  AUDIO: You're building a word web in your brain!
    SCREEN: Build a word web
