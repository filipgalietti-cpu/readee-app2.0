# RI.1.9 — Comparing Two Fact Books  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Two Books, One Topic"
  AUDIO: Two books can be about the same topic.
    SCREEN: Two books, same topic
  AUDIO: But they might share different facts!
    SCREEN: Different facts
  AUDIO: Let us learn how to compare them.

## [teach] heading: "Dogs in Two Books"
  AUDIO: Book one says: dogs need walks.
    SCREEN: Book 1: dogs need walks
  AUDIO: Book two says: dogs need exercise.
    SCREEN: Book 2: dogs need exercise
  AUDIO: These books agree! Walks are one kind of exercise.
    SCREEN: Agree: walks = exercise

## [teach] heading: "Two Books on the Sun"
  AUDIO: Book one says: the sun is hot.
    SCREEN: Book one: sun is hot
  AUDIO: Book two says: the sun gives Earth light.
    SCREEN: Book two: sun gives light
  AUDIO: Both books are about the sun!
    SCREEN: Both about the sun
  AUDIO: Together, we learn even more.

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together! Here are two books about frogs.
  AUDIO: Book one says: frogs jump. Book two says: frogs swim.
    SCREEN: Book 1: frogs jump. Book 2: frogs swim.
  AUDIO: Are both books about the same topic? Yes, both are about frogs!
    SCREEN: Same topic? / Yes, frogs!
  AUDIO: How do these books compare? One tells how it jumps, the other how it swims, so they add facts!
    SCREEN: How do they compare? / They add facts.
  AUDIO: Two books gave us more facts about frogs. You compared like a pro!

## [tip] heading: "Same Topic, More Info"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Reading two books on the same topic gives you more facts than just one!
    SCREEN: Two books = more facts
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to compare fact books!
