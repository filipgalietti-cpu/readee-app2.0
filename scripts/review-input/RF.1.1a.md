# RF.1.1a — What Makes a Sentence  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "What Makes a Sentence"
  AUDIO: Look! Let us talk about sentences.
    SCREEN: Sentences!
  AUDIO: A sentence is a group of words that tells a whole idea.
    SCREEN: A whole idea
  AUDIO: Every sentence has two special rules. Let us learn them!
    SCREEN: Two rules

## [teach] heading: "Rule 1: Start Big"
  AUDIO: Rule one. Every sentence starts with a capital letter.
    SCREEN: Rule 1: Start with a capital
  AUDIO: Look at this sentence. The dog runs.
    SCREEN: The dog runs.
  AUDIO: The first word is The. See the big capital T at the beginning?

## [teach] heading: "Rule 2: End With a Mark"
  AUDIO: Rule two. Every sentence ends with a mark.
  AUDIO: A period. A question mark. Or an exclamation mark!
  AUDIO: A period ends a telling sentence.
    SCREEN: The dog runs.
  AUDIO: A question mark ends a question.
    SCREEN: Is the dog fast?
  AUDIO: And an exclamation mark ends an excited sentence!
    SCREEN: The dog is so fast!

## [example] heading: "Fix It Up"
  AUDIO: Watch. Here is a sentence that is missing its rules.
    SCREEN: the dog runs
  AUDIO: First, make the t a capital T.
    SCREEN: The dog runs
  AUDIO: Then add a period at the end. Now it follows both rules!
    SCREEN: The dog runs.
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to practice! Find the sentences that follow the rules.
    SCREEN: You got it!
