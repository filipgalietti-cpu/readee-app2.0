# RL.1.2 — Retelling Stories  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Retell the Story"
  AUDIO: Retelling means telling a story in your own words.
    SCREEN: Retelling: your own words
  AUDIO: You keep the most important parts.
    SCREEN: Keep important parts
  AUDIO: And you leave out the tiny details.
    SCREEN: Leave out tiny details

## [teach] heading: "Beginning, Middle, End"
  AUDIO: Every story has three big parts.
  AUDIO: The beginning. Who is in the story and what is happening?
    SCREEN: Beginning: Who, what?
  AUDIO: The middle. What is the problem or the big event?
    SCREEN: Middle: Problem, event?
  AUDIO: The end. How does it all work out?
    SCREEN: End: How it works out?

## [example] heading: "Retell Tom's Story"
  AUDIO: Listen. Tom wanted to win the race. He practiced every day. On race day, he came in first!
    SCREEN: Tom wanted to win the race. He practiced every day. On race day, he came in first!
  AUDIO: Beginning. Tom wanted to win a race.
    SCREEN: Tom wanted to win a race.
  AUDIO: Middle. He practiced every single day.
    SCREEN: He practiced every single day.
  AUDIO: End. He won! The lesson? Practice helps!
    SCREEN: Lesson? / Practice helps!

## [tip] heading: "The Story Lesson"
  AUDIO: Many stories teach a lesson.
    SCREEN: Stories teach lessons
  AUDIO: Ask yourself, what did the character learn? That is the lesson.
    SCREEN: What did character learn?
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to practice retelling stories!
