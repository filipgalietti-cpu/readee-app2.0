# RI.2.3 — Connecting History and Science  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Things Are Connected"
  AUDIO: Fact books are full of real events. And those events connect to each other!
    SCREEN: Real events connect
  AUDIO: One thing makes the next thing happen. That is a connection.
    SCREEN: One causes the next
  AUDIO: Let us learn to connect real events in history and science!
    SCREEN: History and science

## [teach] heading: "Cause and Effect"
  AUDIO: A cause is the reason something happens.
    SCREEN: Cause = the reason
  AUDIO: An effect is what happens because of it. The cause leads to the effect.
    SCREEN: Effect = what happens

## [teach] heading: "The Magic Word"
  AUDIO: The word because shows a connection. It links the cause to the effect.
    SCREEN: Look for: because
  AUDIO: Like this. The pond froze because the air got cold.
    SCREEN: Pond froze... / ...because air got cold

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let us try one together. Here is a fact about the sun and rain.
    SCREEN: A real science fact
  AUDIO: The sun heats the water in a lake. The warm water rises up and makes clouds.
    SCREEN: Sun heats water. Water rises to make clouds.
  AUDIO: What is the cause? The sun heats the water!
    SCREEN: Cause? / Sun heats water
  AUDIO: What is the effect? The water rises and makes clouds!
    SCREEN: Effect? / Clouds form
  AUDIO: So clouds form because the sun heats the water. You connected two real ideas!
    SCREEN: Clouds form because of the sun

## [tip] heading: "Ask: Why?"
  AUDIO: Here is a trick. When you read a fact, ask why did this happen?
    SCREEN: Ask: Why did it happen?
  AUDIO: The answer is the cause. Now you can connect any real events!
    SCREEN: The answer is the cause
