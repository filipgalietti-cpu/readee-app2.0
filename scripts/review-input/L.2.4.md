# L.2.4 — Word Meaning Detective  (grade 2nd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Figure It Out"
  AUDIO: Good readers figure out tricky words.
    SCREEN: Figure out words
  AUDIO: They use clues from the sentence and the word itself.
    SCREEN: Use sentence, word clues
  AUDIO: We'll be word meaning detectives today, looking for those clues!

## [teach] heading: "Sentence Clues"
  AUDIO: Read the whole sentence.
    SCREEN: Read whole sentence
  AUDIO: The other words give hints.
    SCREEN: Other words give hints

## [teach] heading: "Word Parts"
  AUDIO: Break the word into pieces. Prefix, root, suffix.
    SCREEN: Break into pieces
  AUDIO: Each piece can tell you part of the meaning.
    SCREEN: Pieces tell meaning

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together, detective. Here's a sentence.
  AUDIO: Mom had to reread the page because she lost her place.
    SCREEN: Mom had to reread the page because she lost her place.
  AUDIO: What word part do you see at the front? Re! And re means again.
    SCREEN: What part? / Re means again!
  AUDIO: So what does reread mean? Read again!
    SCREEN: So reread means? / Read again!
  AUDIO: The part re plus the clue lost her place both point to again. You cracked the case!

## [tip] heading: "Detective Mindset"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Combine sentence clues and word parts. You can almost always figure it out!
    SCREEN: Combine clues, parts
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to be a word meaning detective!
