# K.L.2 — Capitals and Punctuation  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Capitals and Punctuation!"
  AUDIO: When we write, we use special rules to make our writing look right. Like starting with a big letter!
    SCREEN: Start with big letter
  AUDIO: And putting a dot at the end of a sentence. That dot is called a period!
    SCREEN: Dot at end: period
  AUDIO: Let's learn the rules of capitals and punctuation!

## [teach] heading: "Capital Letters"
  AUDIO: Every sentence starts with a capital letter — that's the big, tall version of the letter!
    SCREEN: Sentence starts capital letter
  AUDIO: We also use capitals for names. Like Max, Anna, or your name!
    SCREEN: Capitals for names
  AUDIO: And at the end of a sentence, we put a period. It's like saying 'The end!' for that thought.
    SCREEN: Period ends sentence
  AUDIO: If we're asking a question, we use a question mark! And if we're excited, an exclamation point!
    SCREEN: Question mark, Exclamation point

## [example] heading: "Let's Fix a Sentence!"
  AUDIO: Look at this sentence. Something is missing!
  AUDIO: the cat is big. Hmm — 'the' needs a capital T at the beginning!
    SCREEN: Capital T
  AUDIO: And we need a period at the end! The cat is big. Period! Now it's perfect!
    SCREEN: Period

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: Check every sentence you write for two things!
  AUDIO: Number one: Does it start with a capital letter? Number two: Does it end with punctuation?
    SCREEN: Number one: Does it start with a capital letter? /  Number two: Does it end with punctuation?
  AUDIO: If both answers are yes, your sentence is perfect!
