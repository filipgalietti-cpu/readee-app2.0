# RI.3.3 — Connecting Events and Ideas  (grade 3rd Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Events Connect"
  AUDIO: Events and ideas in nonfiction often connect.
    SCREEN: Events and ideas connect
  AUDIO: Cause and effect. Sequence. Compare and contrast.
    SCREEN: Cause/effect / Sequence / Compare/contrast

## [teach] heading: "Cause and Effect"
  AUDIO: One thing causes another. The water spilled because the glass tipped.
    SCREEN: One thing causes another
  AUDIO: Look for because, so, as a result.
    SCREEN: Signal words

## [teach] heading: "Sequence"
  AUDIO: Sequence is the order of events.
    SCREEN: Sequence = order
  AUDIO: First, next, then, finally.
    SCREEN: First / Next / Then / Finally

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a true event.
  AUDIO: The plant wilted because nobody watered it for a week.
    SCREEN: The plant wilted because nobody watered it.
  AUDIO: What signal word connects them? Because!
    SCREEN: Signal word? / Because.
  AUDIO: What caused it? No water made the plant wilt.
    SCREEN: What's the cause? / No water.
  AUDIO: No water caused the plant to wilt. You connected cause and effect!

## [tip] heading: "Map It Out"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Draw boxes and arrows to show how events connect.
    SCREEN: Draw boxes and arrows
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to connect events and ideas!
