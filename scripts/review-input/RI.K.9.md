# RI.K.9 — Comparing Fact Books  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Compare Fact Books!"
  AUDIO: Did you know two different books can be about the same thing? Like two books about cats!
  AUDIO: But they might tell you different facts! One might talk about what cats eat, and the other about where cats live.
  AUDIO: Let's learn how to compare them!

## [teach] heading: "How Do We Compare Fact Books?"
  AUDIO: When we compare fact books, we look at what's the same and what's different.
  AUDIO: What's the same? Maybe both books are about the same animal or the same topic!
    SCREEN: Same
  AUDIO: What's different? Maybe one book has different facts or different pictures!
    SCREEN: Different

## [teach] heading: "Same Topic, New Facts"
  AUDIO: Two books can share the same topic but still teach you new facts. That is what makes comparing fun.
    SCREEN: Same topic, new facts
  AUDIO: Both books are about frogs, so the topic is the same. But one book says frogs hop, and the other says frogs eat bugs. Same topic, different new facts. Now you have learned two things!
    SCREEN: Both about frogs? / Yes, same!

## [example] heading: "Let's Compare Two Books!"
  AUDIO: Here are two books about bears. Let's see how they're the same and different!
  AUDIO: Book one says bears eat fish. Book two says bears sleep in caves in winter.
    SCREEN: Book one says bears eat fish. Book two says bears sleep in caves in winter.
  AUDIO: What's the same? Both are about bears! What's different? They teach different facts!
    SCREEN: Same? / About bears! Different?

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When you read two books about the same thing, think about this.
  AUDIO: What did I learn from book one? What did I learn from book two? What's the same and what's different?
    SCREEN: Same and different
  AUDIO: Comparing helps you learn even more!
    SCREEN: Learn even more!
