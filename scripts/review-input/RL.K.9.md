# RL.K.9 — Comparing Characters  (grade Kindergarten)

## [intro] heading: "Let's Compare Characters!"
  AUDIO: Have you ever noticed that some characters in stories are kind of alike, and some are really different?
  AUDIO: Today we're going to learn how to compare characters. That means looking at how they're the same and how they're different!
    SCREEN: Same and Different
  AUDIO: This is so fun. Let's do it!

## [teach] heading: "What Does It Mean to Compare?"
  AUDIO: When we compare characters, we look at what's the same about them.
  AUDIO: Maybe they both like the same thing. Or they both go on an adventure!
    SCREEN: Same
  AUDIO: We also look at what's different. Maybe one is brave and the other is shy!
    SCREEN: Different
  AUDIO: Same and different — that's comparing! Easy, right?

## [example] heading: "Let's Try It!"
  AUDIO: Here are two characters. A bear who loves honey, and a bee who makes honey!
  AUDIO: What's the same? They both love honey!
    SCREEN: Same
  AUDIO: What's different? The bear eats honey, but the bee makes honey!
    SCREEN: Different
  AUDIO: See? Comparing is just finding what's the same and what's different. You nailed it!

## [tip] heading: "A Helpful Trick!"
  AUDIO: When you read two stories or meet two characters, think about this.
  AUDIO: First ask: What's the same? What do they share?
    SCREEN: Same?
  AUDIO: Then ask: What's different? What makes each one special?
    SCREEN: Different?
  AUDIO: Those two questions make you a comparing champion!
