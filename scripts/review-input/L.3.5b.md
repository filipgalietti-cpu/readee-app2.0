  AUDIO: Brisk means quick and lively, like a fast walk.

## [teach] heading: "Picture the Moment"

    SCREEN: Picture a real moment
  AUDIO: Reading under a warm blanket feels cozy.
    SCREEN: Warm blanket = / Cozy

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here is a real moment.
    SCREEN: A real moment
  AUDIO: We ran fast in the cool, windy air all morning.
    SCREEN: Running fast in cool wind
  AUDIO: Which word fits this moment, cozy or brisk?
    SCREEN: Cozy or brisk? / Brisk!
  AUDIO: Brisk! Running fast in cool wind feels quick and lively. You matched the word to the moment!
    SCREEN: Why brisk? / Quick and lively
