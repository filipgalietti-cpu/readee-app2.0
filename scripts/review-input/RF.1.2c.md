  AUDIO: Listen! Every word has sounds in a special order.

  AUDIO: Here is the word map.
  AUDIO: The first sound is at the beginning.
  AUDIO: The middle sound is in the middle.
  AUDIO: And the last sound is at the end.

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One Together!"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together! Here is a word.
  AUDIO: The word is bed. Let's stretch it out: b... e... d.
    SCREEN: bed
  AUDIO: What is the first sound? /b/! It comes at the beginning.
    SCREEN: First sound? / /b/!
  AUDIO: And what is the sound in the middle? /e/!
    SCREEN: Middle sound? / /e/!
  AUDIO: First /b/, middle /e/, last /d/. You found every sound in bed!
