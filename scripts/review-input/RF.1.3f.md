# RF.1.3f — Word Endings  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Little Endings Change Meaning"
  AUDIO: Little endings can change what a word means.
    SCREEN: Endings change word meaning
  AUDIO: They tell us how many, or when.
    SCREEN: How many, or when
  AUDIO: Let us meet three of the most common ones.

## [teach] heading: "The -s Ending"
  AUDIO: The S ending means more than one.
    SCREEN: S ending = more than one
  AUDIO: One cat. Two cats.
    SCREEN: One cat, two cats
  AUDIO: Dog becomes dogs. Apple becomes apples.
    SCREEN: Dog becomes dogs

## [teach] heading: "The -ed and -ing Endings"
  AUDIO: The ending E D means it already happened.
    SCREEN: ED = already happened
  AUDIO: Yesterday, I jumped. Already happened!
    SCREEN: Yesterday / I / Jumped
  AUDIO: The ending I N G means happening right now.
    SCREEN: ING = happening now
  AUDIO: I am jumping. Happening now!
    SCREEN: I am jumping.

## [example] heading: "Let's Try One"
  AUDIO: Let's try one together. Here's a word with an ending.
  AUDIO: Here is the word: played.
    SCREEN: played
  AUDIO: What ending do you see? E-D, the -ed ending!
    SCREEN: What ending? / -ed!
  AUDIO: When did it happen? It already happened! The -ed tells us it is done.
    SCREEN: When did it happen? / Already happened!
  AUDIO: So played means it already happened. You read the ending!

## [tip] heading: "Spot the Ending"
  AUDIO: Here is a helpful trick.
  AUDIO: Look at the last letters. The ending tells you when or how many!
    SCREEN: Ending: when or how many
  AUDIO: Now you are ready to read word endings!
