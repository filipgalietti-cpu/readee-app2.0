# RI.1.6 — Pictures vs Words  (grade 1st Grade)

## [intro] heading: "Two Kinds of Clues"
  AUDIO: Fact books give us two kinds of clues.
    SCREEN: Two kinds of clues
  AUDIO: The words tell us facts.
    SCREEN: Words tell facts
  AUDIO: The pictures show us how it looks.

## [teach] heading: "Words Tell, Pictures Show"
  AUDIO: Today, we will learn how words and pictures give us different kinds of information in a book.
  AUDIO: Look at this book about ladybugs; the words tell us that ladybugs are small insects.
    SCREEN: Words tell: small insects
  AUDIO: The picture shows us that ladybugs are red with black spots and have tiny legs.
    SCREEN: Picture shows: red, spots
  AUDIO: So, the words gave us facts we can read, like what kind of animal it is.
    SCREEN: Words give facts
  AUDIO: And the picture gave us details we can see, like its color and shape.
    SCREEN: Pictures give details

## [teach] heading: "What Only Pictures Can Show"
  AUDIO: Some things are hard to tell in words.
    SCREEN: Hard to tell in words
  AUDIO: The exact color of an animal.
    SCREEN: Exact color
  AUDIO: The shape of its spots. How tall it is.
    SCREEN: Shape, size
  AUDIO: Pictures show us those right away!
    SCREEN: Pictures show right away

## [tip] heading: "What Tells What?"
  AUDIO: Sometimes, pictures and words in a book tell us different things.
  AUDIO: Pictures often show us what something looks like or where it is.
    SCREEN: Pictures: what, where
  AUDIO: Words can tell us names, feelings, or why something happened.
    SCREEN: Words tell why
  AUDIO: It's important to notice if the picture or the words are giving you new information.
    SCREEN: Notice new information
  AUDIO: You are now a pro at telling the difference between what pictures and words tell us!
