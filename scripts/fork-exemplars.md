# GOLD-STANDARD FORKS (the quality bar — match this calibre)

## RL.K.1 (Kindergarten) — kind: tap
  Q (spoken): Now you try! Here's a brand-new story. Lily the bird flew to her nest. She had a yellow worm. What did Lily have?
  prompt: "What did Lily have?"
  choices: ['A worm', 'A fish', 'A leaf']  correct: A worm
  affirm: Yes! A worm! You found the key detail all by yourself!
  encourage: So close! Think back to the story — what did Lily have? Try again!
  hint: Think back to the story — what did Lily have?
